Where the map comes from

District lines

U.S. Census Bureau 2024 cartographic boundary files (1:500,000): cb_2024_us_cd119_500k (Massachusetts features only) for the 9 U.S. House districts, cb_2024_25_sldu_500k for the 40 state Senate districts, cb_2024_25_sldl_500k for the 160 state House districts, cb_2024_us_county_500k for counties and cb_2024_25_place_500k for city and town limits. These show the Massachusetts Legislature's 2021 state Senate and House districts. District files are converted without simplification; county outlines and city limits are simplified for display. Near a boundary line they can be off by a block or more.
