Judges on the ballot

Five of the nine seats on the Washington Supreme Court are on the ballot (Positions 1, 3, 4, 5 and 7). Every voter votes on all five. Judicial races are nonpartisan: no party label appears next to a name. Each seat has two candidates in the general election.

We re-checked all five Supreme Court contests against the Secretary of State's election results site on November 4, 2026. The names match. See the section below for Court of Appeals, Superior Court, District Court and municipal court judges. source: WA SOS election results site Names, parties and ballot order are as recorded in our candidate profiles, compiled on October 15, 2026 from the Secretary of State's list. Background lines come from the pages linked on each line. A source marked "(unreviewed)" was not independently reviewed. It links to a news or reference page, not an official one.

County, district and local judges

What this section covers. Every judicial contest on the Washington Secretary of State's election results site for the November 3, 2026 general election: the statewide contest list and the contest list of each of the 39 counties, read on November 4, 2026. That includes Court of Appeals, Superior Court, District Court and the municipal courts that appear on those lists (Seattle and Tacoma). Names are as printed on the ballot.

What it does not cover. Judges who are not on a ballot. The state guide says an uncontested Superior Court race in a county of 100,000 or more people is not printed on the ballot and the candidate gets a certificate of election. Because of that rule, most King, Pierce, Snohomish and Spokane Superior Court judges do not appear here. A candidate who won a majority in the August primary is also not printed in the general. We also did not find municipal courts other than Seattle and Tacoma on the county lists, so other city courts may not be covered. We did not verify who is the incumbent in each race.

Not judges. The Douglas County list also contains Clerk of Superior Court, which is not a judicial seat and is not listed.
