Judges on the ballot

Five of the nine seats on the Washington Supreme Court are on the ballot (Positions 1, 3, 4, 5 and 7). Every voter votes on all five. Judicial races are nonpartisan: no party label appears next to a name. Each seat has two candidates in the general election.

Names, parties and ballot order are as recorded in our candidate profiles, compiled on October 15, 2026 from the Secretary of State's list. Background lines come from the pages linked on each line. A source marked "(unreviewed)" was not independently reviewed. It links to a news or reference page, not an official one.

Supreme Court, Position 1

  • Colleen Melody source: WA Secretary of State, VoteWA listNow: Justice, Washington Supreme Court, Position 1 (since Jan. 2026, appointed). Appointed by Gov. Bob Ferguson; began serving as the 100th justice of the Washington Supreme Court on Jan. 1, 2026. source: courts.wa.gov Was chief of the Civil Rights Division in the Washington State Attorney General's Office for 11 years; earlier a trial attorney in the Civil Rights Division of the U.S. Department of Justice. source: courts.wa.gov Law clerk to Judge Ronald M. Gould of the U.S. Court of Appeals for the Ninth Circuit after law school. source: courts.wa.gov Full profile.
  • Scott Edwards source: WA Secretary of State, VoteWA listNow: Tax attorney. Attorney representing Washington taxpayers for more than 30 years; taught state and local tax in the University of Washington School of Law's LL.M. program for more than 20 years. source: washingtonstatestandard.com (unreviewed) J.D. with honors, University of Washington School of Law (1991); B.A. in economics, Princeton University. source: washingtonstatestandard.com (unreviewed) Has argued cases before the state Supreme Court, including an unsuccessful challenge to the state's capital gains tax. source: newsfromthestates.com (unreviewed) Full profile.

Supreme Court, Position 3

  • David Stevens source: WA Secretary of State, VoteWA listNow: Judge, Mason County Superior Court (elected 2023, re-elected 2024). Mason County Superior Court judge, elected in 2023 and re-elected in 2024. source: spokesman.com (unreviewed) U.S. Navy petty officer, 1983-1989. source: spokesman.com (unreviewed) Spokane County deputy prosecutor (2001-2010); later a federal prosecutor in Sacramento, a U.S. State Department justice adviser in Afghanistan and an EU prosecutor in Kosovo; public defense director for the Confederated Tribes of the Colville Reservation; prosecutor in Okanogan and Mason counties. source: spokesman.com (unreviewed) Full profile.
  • Jaime Michelle Hawk source: WA Secretary of State, VoteWA listNow: Judge, King County Superior Court (appointed 2022, later elected). King County Superior Court judge, appointed by Gov. Jay Inslee in 2022 and later elected. source: spokesman.com (unreviewed) Earlier a juvenile public defender in Grant County, a federal public defender for Eastern Washington and Idaho, a law clerk for a federal judge in Yakima, and legal strategy director for the ACLU of Washington's Smart Justice campaign (from 2015). source: spokesman.com (unreviewed) Began career on U.S. Senate Judiciary Committee staff; former adjunct professor at Gonzaga University School of Law. source: spokesman.com (unreviewed) Full profile.

Supreme Court, Position 4

  • Ian Birk source: WA Secretary of State, VoteWA listNow: Judge, Washington Court of Appeals, Division I (since 2022). Judge on the Washington Court of Appeals, Division I; appointed by Gov. Jay Inslee in 2022 and retained unopposed in 2022. source: washingtonstatestandard.com (unreviewed) Civil litigator in Seattle for about 20 years before joining the bench; clerked for a Washington Supreme Court justice. source: washingtonstatestandard.com (unreviewed) B.A. and J.D., University of Washington. source: washingtonstatestandard.com (unreviewed) Full profile.
