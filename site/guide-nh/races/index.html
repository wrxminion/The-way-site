New Hampshire has 400 state House seats in districts named by county and number (for example "Strafford District 21 - Vote for up to 3"). Names are from the Secretary of State's 2026 General Election candidate list (PDF dated September 17, 2026), as printed, for the 164 county districts in the Census map and for the floterial districts (an extra district laid over several others). Some districts list only one name. A few people are printed twice under different party codes; we flag those instead of merging them. The list has no write-in or withdrawal marks, so a change after September 17 would not show here.
