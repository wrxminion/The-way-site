Statewide offices and U.S. Senate

  • Governor and Lieutenant Governor3 candidates

    Runs the state's executive branch.

  • Attorney General2 candidates

    The state's chief lawyer.

  • Secretary of State4 candidates

    Runs Minnesota's elections: keeps the statewide voter list, approves voting equipment, and leads the board that certifies election results.

  • State Auditor3 candidates

    Watches how local governments (counties, cities, towns and school districts) spend public money: oversees their audits, checks public pension plans and tax increment financing (TIF) districts, and looks into possible theft or misuse of public funds.

  • U.S. Senate4 candidates

    One of Minnesota's two votes in the U.S.

  • Supreme Court, Seat 11 candidates

    Minnesota's highest court.

Minnesota voters choose a governor and lieutenant governor (one ticket), an attorney general, a secretary of state and a state auditor this year. Statewide, voters also choose a U.S. senator, two Supreme Court justices and five Court of Appeals judges, and decide one constitutional amendment.

U.S. House

Your district's one vote in the U.S. House. House members write and vote on federal laws, taxes and spending, and every bill to raise taxes has to start in the House. A term is two years. Minnesota has eight House seats.
