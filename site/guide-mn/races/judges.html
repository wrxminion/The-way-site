Judges on the ballot

Minnesota elects its judges in nonpartisan races. Two Supreme Court seats (Seats 1 and 4) and five Court of Appeals seats (Seats 1, 7, 13, 15 and 18) were on the November 3, 2026 ballot, each with one candidate, the sitting judge. District court judges are also elected, by judicial district.

Updated November 4, 2026. The Secretary of State candidate list (candidates.sos.mn.gov) is behind a captcha and we did not bypass it. Instead we read the Secretary of State statewide results page for all judicial races (unofficial results, 100 percent of precincts reported, last updated 11/04/26 7:58 AM). That page names every candidate who was on the ballot in every judicial race. It is unofficial until the State Canvassing Board meets on November 19, 2026. Our automated fetch tool was turned away by that site; the page loaded normally in a browser with no challenge shown. source: MN SOS unofficial results

Supreme Court

Confirmed on the Secretary of State results page: each seat had one named candidate plus a write-in line. Seat 1 Hennesy received 98.94 percent. Seat 4 Thissen received 99.00 percent. Seat 2 is not listed. source: MN SOS unofficial results

  • Seat 1: Sarah E. Hennesy. Incumbent associate justice. She was the only candidate, so the primary was canceled and she was the only name on the general election ballot. Ballotpedia, MN Supreme Court (unreviewed) source: MN county notice of filing
  • Seat 4: Paul C. Thissen. Incumbent associate justice. He was the only candidate, so the primary was canceled and he was the only name on the general election ballot. Ballotpedia, MN Supreme Court (unreviewed) source: MN county notice of filing
  • Seat 2: not on the ballot. County notices written in April list Theodora Karin Gaïtas for Seat 2. Ballotpedia reports that Gov. Walz named her Chief Justice on May 19, 2026 and named Ramsey County judge Reynaldo Aligada to her seat, and that Seat 2 did not appear on the ballot. We could not check this against the Secretary of State list. Ballotpedia, MN Supreme Court (unreviewed) source: MN county notice of filing

Court of Appeals

Confirmed on the Secretary of State results page: Seats 1, 7, 13, 15 and 18 each had one named candidate (Bentley, Bond, Cochran, Ross, Harris) plus a write-in line. source: MN SOS unofficial results

Appellate seats are elected statewide. Seats 7 and 15 are tied to a congressional district (5th and 3rd), so the candidate must live there. Each seat had one candidate and no primary.

  • Seat 1: Elizabeth G. Bentley. Incumbent judge, only candidate. Ballotpedia, MN appeals (unreviewed) source: MN county notice of filing
  • Seat 7: Rachel Bond. Incumbent judge, only candidate (5th Congressional District seat). Ballotpedia, MN appeals (unreviewed) source: MN county notice of filing
  • Seat 13: Jeanne M. Cochran. Incumbent judge, only candidate. Ballotpedia, MN appeals (unreviewed) source: MN county notice of filing
  • Seat 15: Kevin G. Ross. Incumbent judge, only candidate (3rd Congressional District seat). Ballotpedia, MN appeals (unreviewed) source: MN county notice of filing
  • Seat 18: JaPaul Harris. Incumbent judge, only candidate. Ballotpedia, MN appeals (unreviewed) source: MN county notice of filing

County, district and local judges

Coverage. Minnesota district court judges are elected in nonpartisan races by judicial district. This section covers all ten districts: every district court race listed on the Secretary of State statewide judicial results page, read November 4, 2026 (unofficial results). Names for the 8 districts other than Hennepin and Ramsey were checked against that page and against the April 30 county notice of filing. Contested: three district court races had two candidates: Fifth District Seat 5, Ninth District Seat 2 and Tenth District Seat 38. All other district seats had one named candidate. Not covered: candidate backgrounds, and any seat that does not appear on the results page. Judges are appointed by the governor when a seat opens between elections, then stand for election.
