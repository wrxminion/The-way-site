Candidate · U.S. Senate

Rebecca Whiting

What this job does: One of Minnesota's two votes in the U.S. Senate. Senators write and vote on federal laws, taxes and spending, and approve or reject the president's picks for federal judges, the Supreme Court and the Cabinet. A term is six years.

Libertarian · listed 2 of 4 on the Minnesota Secretary of State's list · on the ballot as "Rebecca Whiting"

Occupation
Bakery owner; U.S. Army veteran
Lives in
Bemidji

Background

  • U.S. Army combat veteran; served two tours in Iraq as a medic, in Ramadi (2004-05) and Baghdad (2006-07). source: lpmn.org
  • Bakery owner and homeschooling parent who lives on a homestead farm near Bemidji. Endorsed by the Libertarian Party of Minnesota. source: lpmn.org
