Governor and Lieutenant Governor

Jared Polis (Democratic) is governor today and cannot run again because of term limits.

What this job does

Colorado's top official. Signs or vetoes bills (and can veto single spending lines), proposes the state budget, names the heads of state agencies with the Senate's OK, and fills openings on Colorado courts from a short list a nominating commission sends. A term is four years, two in a row at most. The lieutenant governor runs on the same ticket and takes over if the governor can't serve. source: ballotpedia.org
