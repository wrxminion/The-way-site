What this job does

One of 65 state representatives in the Colorado General Assembly, which writes state laws and passes the yearly state budget (the "Long Bill") in a session of up to 120 days. A term is two years, four in a row at most. source: en.wikipedia.org

Background

  • Elected to the Colorado House (District 52) in November 2024, succeeding Cathy Kipp; took office January 8, 2025. source: en.wikipedia.org
  • Bachelor of Arts, UC Davis; JD and LLM in taxation, University of San Diego School of Law. source: en.wikipedia.org
  • Tax attorney; co-founder of the Latino Coalition; previously chief deputy assessor of Larimer County. source: en.wikipedia.org
  • Serves on the House Appropriations, Finance and Judiciary committees. source: leg.colorado.gov
