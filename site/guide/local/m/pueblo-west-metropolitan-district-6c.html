PUEBLO WEST METROPOLITAN DISTRICT BALLOT ISSUE 6C

SHALL THE PUEBLO WEST METROPOLITAN DISTRICT WAIVE THE 5.25% PROPERTY TAX LIMIT FOR FISCAL YEAR 2026 AND ALL FUTURE PROPERTY TAX YEARS, AND WITHOUT INCREASING ANY TAX RATE OR IMPOSING ANY NEW TAX, UNLESS SEPARATELY APPROVED BY THE VOTERS, BE PERMITTED TO COLLECT, RETAIN AND SPEND ALL REVENUE IT RECEIVES IN FISCAL YEAR 2026 AND IN ALL PROPERTY TAX YEARS THEREAFTER, AS A VOTER APPROVED REVENUE CHANGE AND PROPERTY TAX REVENUE CHANGE UNDER ARTICLE X, SECTION 20 OF THE COLORADO CONSTITUTION, AND BE PERMITTED TO EXCEED THE 5.5% PROPERTY TAX REVENUE GROWTH LIMITATION CONTAINED IN SECTION 29-1-301, COLORADO REVISED STATUTES, THE 5.25% PROPERTY TAX LIMIT CONTAINED IN SECTION 29-1-1702, COLORADO REVISED STATUTES, AND ANY OTHER REVENUE LIMITATION CONTAINED IN THE LAWS OF THE STATE, UTILIZING ALL OF THE EXCESS REVENUE OVER SAID LIMITS FOR THE SOLE PURPOSE OF FUNDING THE PUEBLO WEST METROPOLITAN DISTRICT PARKS AND RECREATION DEPARTMENT?

  • NO / AGAINST
