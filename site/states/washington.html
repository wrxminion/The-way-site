Voting in Washington

General election: Tuesday, November 3, 2026.

At a glance

Register
Online, by mail or in person

Many people are registered automatically when they deal with the DMV or certain state agencies. You can register and vote on the same day, including Election Day.

ID at the polls
ID asked for

Show photo ID or other ID when voting in person (most vote by mail with signature check); without it, vote a provisional ballot counted if your signature matches. No ID? Ask your election office whether it offers a free one for voting. First-time voters who registered by mail may need to show ID under federal law.

Mail voting
Every voter gets a ballot by mail

Ballot mailed automatically to every registered voter; no request needed Return deadline: postmarked by Election Day and received by Nov 23 (day before county certification on Nov 24).

Early in-person voting
Yes

Voting centers Oct 16 - 8 p.m. Nov 3 (18 days before)

After a conviction
Voting rights return when you leave prison

Since 2022 only people currently in prison for a felony lose the vote; restored automatically on release.

Where the road gets harder

Every rule is a cost, and it doesn't fall on everyone the same. A deadline, a document or a trip to an office is easy for some people and a real obstacle for others: people who work hourly jobs, move often, live far from an office, don't drive, or have a disability. Here is what that looks like in Washington.

  • No extra hurdles beyond the basics stood out in our check. Register early anyway, and confirm your registration before Election Day.
