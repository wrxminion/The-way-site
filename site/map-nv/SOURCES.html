Where the map comes from

District lines

U.S. Census Bureau 2024 cartographic boundary files (1:500,000): cb_2024_us_cd119_500k (Nevada features only) for the 4 U.S. House districts, cb_2024_32_sldu_500k for the 21 state Senate districts, cb_2024_32_sldl_500k for the 42 state House districts, cb_2024_us_county_500k for counties and cb_2024_32_place_500k for city and town limits. These show the {'url': 'https://www.leg.state.nv.us/Division/Research/Districts/Reapp/2021/', 'label': 'Nevada Legislature: 2021 district maps', '_check': 'URL not opened this session'}. District files are converted without simplification; county outlines and city limits are simplified for display. Near a boundary line they can be off by a block or more.
