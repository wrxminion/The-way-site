Judges on the ballot

We did not find a statewide judge on Oregon's November 3, 2026 ballot. Oregon's nonpartisan Supreme Court and Court of Appeals races were on the May 19, 2026 ballot, where a candidate with more than half the vote wins outright.

The Oregon Secretary of State's candidate filing search shows no Supreme Court or Court of Appeals candidate in the November general election. Circuit court judges are on the November ballot in five counties. See the section below. source: Oregon SOS ORESTAR search

What we found

  • May ballot. The League of Women Voters of Oregon's primary guide lists the Supreme Court and Court of Appeals races (Positions 1, 9, 12 and 13) among the May 19 contests. source: lwvor.org (unreviewed)
  • Incumbents named. Ballotpedia names Judge Ryan O'Connor (Position 1) and Judge Erin Lagesen (Position 12) as incumbents seeking election. Results are not confirmed here. source: ballotpedia.org (unreviewed)

County, district and local judges

What this section covers. Every candidate for Judge of the Circuit Court in the 2026 General Election in the Oregon Secretary of State's ORESTAR candidate filing search, read on November 4, 2026. It returned 12 candidates for 7 seats in 5 counties. We matched them to the state's list of offices open for the general election (revised July 2, 2026) and, for Multnomah County, the county's own ballot content report dated September 22, 2026.

What it does not cover. Oregon circuit judges are elected in the May primary. A candidate with more than half the vote wins outright, so most circuit seats in all 36 counties are not on the November ballot and are not listed. Voters in other counties may see no judge. We did not check the Oregon Tax Court or the county sample ballots of the other counties.

Supreme Court and Court of Appeals. The same ORESTAR search returned no candidates for the Oregon Supreme Court or the Court of Appeals in the November general election, and neither appears on the state's open offices list. Ballotpedia lists a Supreme Court seat for November, which conflicts. We rely on the Secretary of State.

Ballot format. Judicial races are nonpartisan. Incumbency is shown only where the county list marks it.
