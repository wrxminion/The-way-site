Candidate · Governor

Christine Drazan

What this job does: The Governor is the head of Oregon's state government for a 4-year term. The Governor signs or vetoes bills, proposes the state budget, runs state agencies, and appoints many officials and judges to fill open seats.

Republican, Libertarian · listed 3 of 3 on the Oregon Secretary of State's list · on the ballot as "Christine Drazan"

Current office
State Senator
Occupation
State Senator

Background

  • Pamphlet lists occupation as State Senator; occupational background State Representative and Executive Director. source: sos.oregon.gov
  • Education listed: George Fox University, BA. source: sos.oregon.gov
  • Prior government experience listed: House Republican Leader; Oregon Innovation Council; Clackamas County Planning Commission. source: sos.oregon.gov
  • Was the Republican nominee for governor in 2022. Says she grew up in southern Oregon (Klamath Falls) and once worked as a staffer at the state Capitol. source: katu.com

In their own words

What the candidate says they will work on, from their campaign site or their answers to news-outlet questionnaires. We list it as stated and do not rate it.

  • "The Drazan Plan" lists: "Lower the Cost of Living," "Reignite Oregon's Economy," "Break the Cycle of Homelessness and Addiction," "Unlock the Door to Homeownership," "Restore Graduation Requirements," "Audit Spending and Deliver Results." source: christinefororegon.com
  • Said: "I will balance the budget without new taxes." source: katu.com
  • Said Oregon's sanctuary laws "have gone too far" and wants more cooperation with law enforcement. source: katu.com
