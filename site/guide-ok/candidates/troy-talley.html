Candidate · State House, District 81

Troy Talley

What this job does: Your district's vote in the Oklahoma House of Representatives. Members write and vote on state laws and the state budget; bills to raise taxes start in the House. A term is two years.

Republican · listed 1 of 2 on the State Election Board's list · on the ballot as "TROY TALLEY"

Occupation
Senior account manager, Chickasaw Telecom
Lives in
Edmond

Background

  • Earned a business degree from the University of Central Oklahoma (then Central State) in 1986; wrestled on three UCO national championship teams. source: troytalley.com
  • Has worked in the technology and telecommunications sectors in central Oklahoma; senior account manager at Chickasaw Telecom. source: troytalley.com
  • Grew up in LeFlore County (Poteau) and moved to Edmond for college. source: troytalley.com
  • Deacon at Henderson Hills Baptist Church, a member for more than 33 years. source: troytalley.com
  • Won the August 25, 2026 Republican runoff for the open House District 81 seat. source: theballotbrief.com
