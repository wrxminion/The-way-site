Election Studies

An election study explains the policy behind a ballot decision: the rule, how it came to be, how it has changed, what the research finds, and where every fact comes from. They tie directly to the measures on your ballot.

Every election study says how far it has been reviewed. None is a recommendation for or against a measure.
