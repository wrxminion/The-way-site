Judges on the ballot

No Idaho Supreme Court or Court of Appeals seat is on the November 3, 2026 ballot. All three were decided on May 19, 2026, and each incumbent ran unopposed. One Ada County district judge race goes to a November runoff, and county magistrate judges face yes or no retention votes.

Idaho elects judges in nonpartisan races held with the May primary. A race goes to the November general election only if nobody wins a majority in May. We checked the Secretary of State's judicial seat list and Ballotpedia. We could not open the Secretary of State's November candidate search, so the magistrate list below is not complete.

Statewide courts: decided May 19

  • Supreme Court, Seat C. Justice Gregory Moeller was the only candidate and won with 216,640 votes. source: Ballotpedia
  • Supreme Court, Seat E. Justice Cynthia Meyer was the only candidate and won with 214,856 votes. source: Ballotpedia
  • Court of Appeals, Seat D. Judge Michael Tribe was the only candidate and won with 213,089 votes. source: Ballotpedia
  • Which seats were up. The Secretary of State lists Supreme Court Seats C and E and Court of Appeals Seat D as up for election in 2026. source: Idaho Secretary of State

Ada County (4th Judicial District): district judge runoff

  • Ada County district judge seat vacated by Judge Lynn Norton. Jeffrey Street Jr. (40.65%, 29,120 votes) and Greg Woodard (30.56%, 21,896 votes) advance to a November runoff. David Morse (28.79%) did not advance. Percentages are unofficial May results. The Capital Sun says the seat came open because Norton did not seek re-election. source: Idaho Capital Sun
  • Other Ada district seats. The Secretary of State's seat list shows all twelve Ada district seats (A to L) up in 2026. Only the runoff above is reported as going to November. We did not find results for the other eleven seats. source: Idaho Secretary of State

Other district courts: decided in May

  • 7th District. District Judge Steven Boyce won 70.36% over Bonneville County Prosecutor Randy Neal (29.64%), unofficial. source: Idaho Capital Sun
  • 6th District. District Judge Cody Brower won over Magistrate Judge Aaron Thompson, according to East Idaho News. source: East Idaho News
  • 1st District. The Secretary of State list shows Kootenai Seats A, B, D and E, Bonner A and B and Shoshone A among seats up in 2026. A Coeur d'Alene Press headline says Allen swept the 1st District judge race. We did not read the details, so we name no result. source: Idaho Secretary of State source: Coeur d'Alene Press (unreviewed)
  • 3rd District. The Secretary of State list shows Canyon Seats A to G up in 2026. We did not find Canyon results or any Canyon runoff. source: Idaho Secretary of State

Magistrate judges: retention vote in November

  • How it works. Idaho magistrates stand for retention in the November general election. The ballot asks whether the named magistrate of a county should be retained in office. A majority of yes votes is needed to keep the seat. source: Ballotpedia source: Idaho Code Title 1, Ch. 22
  • Who is on your ballot. The unofficial names we found are in the next section. Your county sample ballot is the official list. source: VoteIdaho sample ballot
