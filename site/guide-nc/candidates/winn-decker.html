Candidate · State House, District 37

Winn Decker

What this job does: Your district's vote in the N.C. House of Representatives. Members write and vote on state laws and the state budget, and propose constitutional amendments like the ones on this ballot. A term is two years.

Democrat · listed 1 of 2 on the State Board of Elections' list · on the ballot as "Winn Decker"

Occupation
Education policy strategist (senior manager at Common App)
Lives in
Fuquay-Varina

Background

  • Works as a senior manager at Common App, a nonprofit college application platform. source: indyweek.com
  • Ph.D. in public administration from North Carolina State University. source: indyweek.com
  • Previously worked at The Hunt Institute on education and workforce policy with governors' offices, state agencies and legislators. source: indyweek.com
  • Won the March 2026 Democratic primary for House District 37; first run for office. source: indyweek.com
