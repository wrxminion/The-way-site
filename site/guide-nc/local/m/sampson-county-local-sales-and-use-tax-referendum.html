Local measure · Sampson County

Sampson County Local Sales and Use Tax ReferendumAdd a 0.5% county sales tax

What your vote means

YES

Sampson County adds a 0.5% sales tax (50 cents on a $100 purchase).

NO

The county sales tax stays where it is.

Our one-line summary of the official wording.

In plain words

Our summary of the official materials and local reporting.

  • Adds a 0.5% county sales tax on top of the sales taxes already charged. That is 50 cents more on a $100 purchase. source: ncsbe.gov (State Board of Elections)
  • The official wording says how the money must be used: to be used only for public education. source: ncsbe.gov (State Board of Elections)

Cost: 50 cents more on every $100 of taxable purchases in Sampson County.
