No governor or other statewide executive office is on the ballot in North Carolina this year. Statewide, voters choose a U.S. senator, one N.C. Supreme Court justice and three N.C. Court of Appeals judges. Judges in North Carolina run in partisan races, like other offices.

U.S. House

Your district's one vote in the U.S. House. House members write and vote on federal laws, taxes and spending, and every bill to raise taxes has to start in the House. A term is two years.
