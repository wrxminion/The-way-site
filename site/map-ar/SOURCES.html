Where the map comes from

District lines

U.S. Census Bureau 2024 cartographic boundary files (1:500,000): cb_2024_us_cd119_500k (Arkansas features only) for the 4 U.S. House districts, cb_2024_05_sldu_500k for the 35 state Senate districts, cb_2024_05_sldl_500k for the 100 state House districts, cb_2024_us_county_500k for counties and cb_2024_05_place_500k for city and town limits. These show the {'url': 'https://gis.arkansas.gov/2021/11/30/2021-arkansas-state-and-house-redistricting-district-maps-data-publication/', 'label': 'Arkansas GIS Office: 2021 state Senate and House district maps'}. District files are converted without simplification; county outlines and city limits are simplified for display. Near a boundary line they can be off by a block or more.
