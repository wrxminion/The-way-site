Local measure · Duval County

Duval County School District DUVAL COUNTY SCHOOL DISTRICT AD VALOREM MILLAGE ELECTION

What your vote means

YES

A YES vote keeps the 1-mill school property tax in place from July 1, 2027 through June 30, 2031.

NO

A NO vote lets the 1-mill school property tax end when the current approval runs out.

Our one-line summary of the official wording.

In plain words

Our summary of the official materials and local reporting.

  • This asks voters to keep the school district's extra 1-mill property tax for four more years, from July 2027 to June 2031. source: jaxtoday.org
  • Voters first approved this tax in 2022. The district says it adds about $8,000 to each teacher's pay and also pays for arts and athletics. source: jaxtoday.org
  • The money is shared with charter schools, and the district must report each year on how it is spent. source: jaxtoday.org
  • Money: 1 mill = $1 for every $1,000 of taxable value; $100 a year on a $100,000 taxable home (Illustration only). Jacksonville Today estimates about $300 a year for the average Jacksonville homeowner. source: jaxtoday.org
