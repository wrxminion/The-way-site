Candidate · Governor and Lieutenant Governor

Charles Burkett

What this job does: Florida's top official. Signs or vetoes the bills the Legislature passes, can cross out single items in the state budget, picks the leaders of most state agencies, and appoints judges from short lists a commission sends. Leads the response to hurricanes and other emergencies. A term is four years, two in a row at most.

No Party Affiliation · listed 5 of 8 on the Division of Elections' list · on the ballot as "Charles Burkett"

Occupation
Real estate investor; former Mayor of Surfside
Lives in
Surfside

Background

  • B.S. in finance from the University of Miami. source: burkettforgov.com
  • Real estate investor since 1983; owns a national investment company, per campaign site. source: burkettforgov.com
  • Chaired the Miami Beach Housing Authority for nine years. source: burkettforgov.com
  • Elected Mayor of Surfside in 2006 and re-elected in 2008, 2020 and 2024 (four terms). source: burkettforgov.com
  • Was Surfside mayor during the 2021 Champlain Towers South collapse. source: wlrn.org
  • Entered the governor's race in May 2026 with no party affiliation. source: wlrn.org
