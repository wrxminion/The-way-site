Candidate · Governor and Lieutenant Governor

Byron Donalds

What this job does: Florida's top official. Signs or vetoes the bills the Legislature passes, can cross out single items in the state budget, picks the leaders of most state agencies, and appoints judges from short lists a commission sends. Leads the response to hurricanes and other emergencies. A term is four years, two in a row at most.

Republican · listed 1 of 8 on the Division of Elections' list · on the ballot as "Byron Donalds"

Current office
U.S. Representative, Florida's 19th Congressional District (since 2021)
Occupation
Congressman; former banking and financial services professional
Lives in
Naples

Background

  • Born in Brooklyn, New York. source: ballotpedia.org
  • Earned a bachelor's degree in finance and marketing from Florida State University in 2002. source: ballotpedia.org
  • Worked as a commercial credit officer in banking and as a portfolio manager in financial services. source: ballotpedia.org
  • Served in the Florida House of Representatives, District 80, from 2016 to 2020. source: ballotpedia.org
  • Has represented Florida's 19th Congressional District in the U.S. House since 2021. source: ballotpedia.org
  • Won the Republican primary for governor on August 18, 2026. source: wusf.org
