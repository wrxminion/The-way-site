Judges on the ballot

Mississippi elects its judges in nonpartisan races with no primary. Five Court of Appeals seats and many chancery and circuit court seats are on the November 3, 2026 ballot. No Supreme Court seat is on this ballot.

Checked on November 4, 2026 against the Mississippi Secretary of State's Candidate Qualifying List (all offices and candidates on file). The list says the State Board of Election Commissioners decides who goes on the ballot, so it can differ from your sample ballot. Mississippi judicial races are nonpartisan and have no primary.

Supreme Court

The Secretary of State's qualifying list has no Supreme Court section for the Nov. 3, 2026 election. We found no Supreme Court seat on this ballot.

County, district and local judges

What this covers. Every Chancery Court and Circuit Court judge on the Secretary of State's Candidate Qualifying List as of Nov. 4, 2026: 79 chancery names in 59 places and 78 circuit names. Races are nonpartisan. Mississippi districts cover several counties, so each entry shows the district and the counties from the Mississippi Judiciary's court lists. A place with one name is unopposed on that list. Not covered: county court, municipal and justice court judges (the list has no county court section and we did not find an official list for the others), and any candidate added or removed after this list was read. We did not profile any candidate. Largest counties are first: Hinds, DeSoto, Harrison, Rankin and Madison, and Jackson.
