Candidate · U.S. Senate

Cindy Hyde-Smith

What this job does: A U.S. Senator helps write and vote on federal laws, votes on the federal budget, and votes to approve or reject the President's picks for judges and top officials. Mississippi has 2 senators. Each serves a 6-year term.

Republican · listed 2 of 3 on the Mississippi Secretary of State's list · on the ballot as "Cindy Hyde-Smith"

Current office
U.S. Senator from Mississippi (since April 2018)
Occupation
U.S. Senator; family raises beef cattle
Lives in
Brookhaven

Background

  • Graduated from Copiah-Lincoln Community College and the University of Southern Mississippi. source: hydesmith.senate.gov
  • Served 12 years in the Mississippi State Senate, including eight as chair of the Agriculture Committee. source: hydesmith.senate.gov
  • Elected Mississippi Commissioner of Agriculture and Commerce in 2011 and reelected in 2015. source: hydesmith.senate.gov
  • Appointed to the U.S. Senate in April 2018 to fill the seat left by Thad Cochran; won the November 2018 special election and a six-year term in November 2020. source: hydesmith.senate.gov
  • Serves on the Appropriations; Agriculture, Nutrition, and Forestry; Energy and Natural Resources; and Rules and Administration committees. source: hydesmith.senate.gov
  • Won the March 10 2026 Republican primary with 80.8% of the vote over Sarah Adlakha. source: en.wikipedia.org
