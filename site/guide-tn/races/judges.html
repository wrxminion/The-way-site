Judges on the ballot

We did not find any state appellate judge on Tennessee's November 3, 2026 general election ballot. Tennessee's Supreme Court and appeals judges face a yes-or-no retention vote in August.

The 2026 statewide judicial retention election was held on August 6, 2026. A county election commission posted its certified results.

What we found

  • Retention election. Weakley County's certification shows county totals for Supreme Court, Court of Appeals and Court of Criminal Appeals judges in the State Judicial Retention Election held August 6, 2026. We did not find certified statewide totals. source: Weakley County Election Commission
  • Law. Tennessee Code section 17-1-103 provides that appellate judges are elected by statewide retention vote. source: syfert.com (unreviewed)

County, district and local judges

Coverage, as of November 4, 2026. We read the official November 3, 2026 sample ballots of the three biggest counties: Davidson (Nashville), Shelby (Memphis) and Knox. We searched each for judge races and found none. The Shelby and Knox files are labeled sample ballots, so final ballots could differ. We did not read the sample ballots of the other 92 counties, so we cannot rule out a local judicial race there. Not covered: municipal court judges, who in some cities are elected or appointed under city charters we did not check.

  • Davidson County, November 3
    No judge races on the sample ballot source: Davidson Election Commission    The sample ballot for the State and Federal General Election lists constitutional amendments and candidates, with no circuit, chancery, criminal, general sessions or other judge contest.
  • Shelby County, November 3
    No judge races on the sample ballot source: Shelby Election Commission    The official sample ballot lists state, federal and municipal races. We found no county or state judge contest on it.
  • Knox County, November 3
    No judge races on the sample ballot source: Knox Election Commission    The revised precinct sample ballot lists amendments, a city charter question and legislative races. We found no judge contest on it.

Where Tennessee trial judges were on the ballot: August 6

Tennessee elects trial judges at the August county general election. These seats were on the August 6, 2026 ballot, so voters have already decided them. We name the seats only, not the results.

  • Shelby County, August 6
    Circuit Court Division III (District 30), Criminal Court Divisions I and VIII, General Sessions Civil Division 2, General Sessions Criminal Division 7 source: Shelby Election Commission    These judge contests appear in the commission's tabulation of votes for the August 6 election. Certified totals are in that file.
  • Knox County, August 6
    General Sessions Division IV (unexpired term) and Criminal Court Division I, District 6 (unexpired term) source: Knox Election Commission    Both appear on the county's August 6 sample ballot.
