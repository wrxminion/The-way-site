Where the map comes from

District lines

U.S. Census Bureau 2024 cartographic boundary files (1:500,000): the NC General Assembly's 2025 congressional plan shapefile (S.L. 2025-95, from ncleg.gov), simplified slightly for display, for the 14 U.S. House districts, cb_2024_37_sldu_500k for the 50 state Senate districts, cb_2024_37_sldl_500k for the 120 state House districts, cb_2024_us_county_500k for counties and cb_2024_37_place_500k for city and town limits. These show the North Carolina General Assembly's 2023 state Senate and House maps. District files are converted without simplification; county outlines and city limits are simplified for display. Near a boundary line they can be off by a block or more.
