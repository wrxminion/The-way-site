amendment 9Raise the income limit for the property tax freeze to $150,000

Legislatively referred constitutional amendment: Amendment No. 9 (Act 220 (2025), HB 300), placed on the ballot by the Legislature as Amendment No. 9 (Act 220 (2025), HB 300) ↗. Passes with Majority of votes cast statewide.

Read the full text of the measure: Official amendment list (PDF) ↗

In plain words

Raises the income limit for the property assessment freeze (for seniors and some others) from $100,000 to $150,000. (house.louisiana.gov ↗)

What your vote means

YES

A YES vote raises the income limit for the freeze to $150,000, adjusted for inflation starting in 2028, with income checked against state tax returns.

NO

A NO vote keeps the limit at $100,000, adjusted for inflation.

Our one-line summary. Louisiana does not publish an official "what your vote means" statement; the ballot title above is the official description.

Why people want it, and why others object

Why this is on the ballot

The Legislature put this on the ballot. It changes the state constitution, so only another statewide vote can change it back. It passes with Majority of votes cast statewide.

  • Louisiana Legislature, by two-thirds vote of each chamber (Act 220 (2025), HB 300; sponsor Rep. Shane Mack) source: sos.la.gov
  • The limit was $50,000 in 2001 and was raised to $100,000 by voters in 2020. Nearly 233,000 people got special assessments in 2025 (PAR). This amendment was passed in the 2025 session. source: parlouisiana.org
