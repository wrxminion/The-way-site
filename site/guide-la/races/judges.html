Judges on the ballot

No Louisiana Supreme Court race is on the Nov. 3, 2026 ballot: all three seats were settled earlier in the year. The contested judge races on that ballot are for district, parish, city and Orleans Parish courts, listed below.

Checked on November 4, 2026 against the Louisiana Secretary of State's election data for the Nov. 3 ballot and Wikipedia's account of the May 16 Supreme Court result. Louisiana judges serve long terms, so the Supreme Court seats were decided earlier this year, not on Nov. 3.

Supreme Court

No Louisiana Supreme Court race is on the Nov. 3 ballot. Three seats were up in 2026. Two were filled without a vote. The third was decided in the May 16 Republican party primary.

Courts of Appeal

No Court of Appeal race appears in the Secretary of State's list of contested Nov. 3 races. A seat with only one candidate is filled at qualifying and never reaches the ballot, so a sitting appeals judge may still have won a term this year without a vote. We did not find an official list of those.

County, district and local judges

What this covers. Every judge race on the Secretary of State's Nov. 3, 2026 election data (unofficial, written Nov. 4): 31 district court seats, 6 New Orleans court seats, 18 parish, family, city and magistrate court seats, and 79 justice of the peace seats. Louisiana holds an open primary on Nov. 3 and a runoff on Dec. 12 if no one wins a majority. Not covered: any judge who drew no opponent. Those judges were elected at qualifying in August and are not on the ballot, and we found no official list of them. Candidates are shown with the party label the Secretary of State prints, and several races are between candidates of one party. We did not profile any candidate.
