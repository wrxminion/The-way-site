Where the map comes from

District lines

U.S. Census Bureau 2024 cartographic boundary files (1:500,000): cb_2024_us_cd119_500k (Virginia features only) for the 11 U.S. House districts, cb_2024_51_sldu_500k for the 40 state Senate districts, cb_2024_51_sldl_500k for the 100 state House districts, cb_2024_us_county_500k for counties and cb_2024_51_place_500k for city and town limits. These show the Supreme Court of Virginia's 2021 House of Delegates map (only District 20 has a race this year). District files are converted without simplification; county outlines and city limits are simplified for display. Near a boundary line they can be off by a block or more.
