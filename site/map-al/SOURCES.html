Where the map comes from

District lines

U.S. Census Bureau 2024 cartographic boundary files (1:500,000): cb_2024_us_cd119_500k (Alabama features only) for the 7 U.S. House districts, cb_2024_01_sldu_500k for the 35 state Senate districts, cb_2024_01_sldl_500k for the 105 state House districts, cb_2024_us_county_500k for counties and cb_2024_01_place_500k for city and town limits. These show the Alabama Legislature's state House and Senate maps (2021 plans as amended; a federal court cleared the 2021 Senate map for Senate Districts 25 and 26 in 2026, with special primaries held Aug. 11). District files are converted without simplification; county outlines and city limits are simplified for display. Near a boundary line they can be off by a block or more.
