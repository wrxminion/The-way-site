Candidate · Governor and Lieutenant Governor

Andy Ellis

What this job does: Runs the state government. The governor proposes the state budget each year, signs or vetoes bills, and appoints the heads of state agencies and the judges of the top courts (with the state Senate's approval). The governor and lieutenant governor are elected together as one ticket. A term is four years.
