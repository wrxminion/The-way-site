Local measure · Howard County

Howard County Question ACharter Amendment – Gender Neutrality

What your vote means

YES

A YES vote changes words like "he" and "she" in the County Charter to gender-neutral words like "they."

NO

A NO vote leaves the gender-specific words in the Charter as they are.

Our one-line summary of the official wording.

In plain words

Our summary of the official materials and local reporting.

  • The change would replace words like "he," "she," "his," and "her" in the Charter with gender-neutral words. source: cc.howardcountymd.gov
  • The question is on the ballot because it changes the Charter, which voters must approve. source: elections.maryland.gov

Cost: The county estimates about $2,600 to update the Charter, to be paid from the Office of Law's operating budget if the County Executive's FY27 budget includes it (source: Council fiscal analysis of CR23-2026).

Why people want it, and why others object

Why this is on the ballot

  • The Howard County LGBTQIA+ Commission sent a 2025 letter to the County Council and County Executive recommending a review of the Charter's language. The Council then put the change on the ballot through Council Resolution 23-2026. source: cc.howardcountymd.gov

Why supporters want it

  • The resolution's sponsors say the change would make the Charter reflect the diversity of the officials, employees, and residents it governs, and make every provision apply equally regardless of gender identity. They note Anne Arundel, Baltimore, and Prince George's counties adopted similar language. source: cc.howardcountymd.gov

Word for word from the State Board of Elections' official Howard ballot: elections.maryland.gov ↗.

Charter Amendment Gender Neutrality
The County Charter uses gender-specific words like "he" and "she". The Charter Amendment would replace these types of words with gender-neutral words like "they" and "them". A vote for the Amendment would make gender-neutral changes to the Charter. A vote against the Amendment would leave gender-specific words unchanged in the Charter.
