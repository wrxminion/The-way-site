amendment 1Only U.S. citizens can vote

Legislatively referred constitutional amendment: House Concurrent Resolution 5004 (2025 Session Laws of Kansas, Chapter 136), placed on the ballot by the Legislature as House Concurrent Resolution 5004 (2025 Session Laws of Kansas, Chapter 136) ↗. Passes with A simple majority of the votes cast on the question (more YES than NO)..

Read the full text of the measure: Official ballot language ↗

In plain words

Right now the Kansas Constitution says every U.S. citizen who is 18 or older and lives in the voting area is a qualified voter. This amendment flips the wording: no one is a qualified voter unless they are a U.S. citizen, 18 or older, and live in the voting area. Federal law already bars noncitizens from voting in federal races. (kslegislature.gov ↗)

Biggest change

  • New wording: 'No person shall be deemed a qualified elector unless such person: (1) Is a citizen of the United States; (2) has attained the age of eighteen years; and (3) ... resides in the voting area in which such person seeks to vote.' source: sos.ks.gov
  • Keeps the current rules on voting for president and vice president after moving out of state, and on voting in a former voting area after moving inside Kansas. source: sos.ks.gov

Who it affects most: Mostly a wording change. It would matter most if a Kansas city or county ever tried to let noncitizens vote in local elections.

Cost to the state: No fiscal note was available. The Secretary of State's office estimated about $50,000 from the State General Fund to publish public notice of the amendment.

What your vote means

YES

A YES vote changes Article 5, Section 1 of the Kansas Constitution to say only U.S. citizens who are 18 or older and live in the voting area can vote.

NO

A NO vote keeps the current wording of Article 5, Section 1 the same.

Our one-line summary. Kansas does not publish an official "what your vote means" statement; the ballot title above is the official description.

Why people want it, and why others object

Why this is on the ballot

The Legislature put this on the ballot. It changes the state constitution, so only another statewide vote can change it back. It passes with A simple majority of the votes cast on the question (more YES than NO)..

  • The Kansas Legislature, by a two-thirds vote in each chamber (House 90-28 on Feb 5, 2025; Senate 37-3 on Mar 25, 2025). The governor has no role. source: sos.ks.gov
  • Rep. Pat Proctor (R-Leavenworth) and 46 other House members sponsored the resolution. source: kslegislature.gov
  • Federal law already bars noncitizens from voting in federal elections. Per Ballotpedia, noncitizens may vote in some local elections in three states. source: kcur.org
  • Eight states approved similar citizenship-voting amendments in 2024, including Iowa, Kentucky, and South Carolina. source: news.ballotpedia.org
  • A different amendment, to have voters elect Kansas Supreme Court justices (SCR 1611), was on the Aug 4, 2026 primary ballot and was rejected. It is not on the Nov 3 ballot. source: kspress.com
