Constitutional Amendments

Every Georgia voter decides these 3. Each page has the official ballot title word for word, what a yes or no vote does, and links to the full text.

  • Amendment 1: Bigger farm and timber land tax break Double the farm and timber land that can get a lower property tax value, from 2,000 to 4,000 acresmajority to pass

    Biggest change: Doubles the conservation use acreage limit for one owner from 2,000 to 4,000 acres.

  • Amendment 2: Probate judges run without a party label Probate judges run in nonpartisan races, with no party next to their namesmajority to pass

    Biggest change: Probate judges would be the last Georgia judges to switch to nonpartisan races.

  • Amendment 3: A state fund for modern 911 Lets lawmakers put existing 911 money in a fund used only for a modern statewide 911 systemmajority to pass

    Biggest change: Creates a fund whose money can only go to building, keeping up and running 911 systems in Georgia, and that does not lapse at the end of the budget year..

All three were placed on the ballot by the General Assembly with a two-thirds vote in each chamber and amend the Georgia Constitution. Each passes with a simple majority of the votes cast on it. Source: Secretary of State: 2026 Constitutional Amendments summaries booklet (PDF) ↗.
