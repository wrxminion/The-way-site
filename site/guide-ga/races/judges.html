Judges on the ballot

We did not find any state judge or justice on Georgia's November 3, 2026 ballot. Georgia's nonpartisan judicial elections were held with the May 19, 2026 primary.

Checked November 4, 2026. We opened the Georgia Secretary of State unofficial results page for the November 3, 2026 General and Special Elections (last updated 11/04/26 2:19 AM) and searched its contest list for the words Court, Judge, Justice and Probate. The search found no judge or court contest. The same page lists three Proposed Constitutional Amendments. One of them is the probate judge question described below. Georgia holds nonpartisan judicial elections with the May primary, not in November. The Secretary of State calendar and 2026 amendment booklet returned access errors from our build, so we did not read them. Check your sample ballot. source: GA SOS unofficial results

County, district and local judges

Coverage, as of November 4, 2026 (third pass). Confirmed on official county sample ballots for November 3: Fulton, DeKalb, Gwinnett, Cobb and Richmond (Augusta) counties list no judge or justice contest. Gwinnett and Cobb show a Solicitor General race, which is a prosecutor, not a judge. Not reached: Chatham, Clayton, Cherokee, Forsyth and Henry county sample ballots (the county pages gave errors or no ballot file), and the Secretary of State pages that block our build. The Chatham County Board of Elections page says two special elections are on its November 3 ballot (a school tax referendum and a school board vacancy); it names no judge seat. Municipal and city courts were not checked city by city. Runoffs: Georgia lists a December 1, 2026 general and special runoff date; we did not find a judge seat in any runoff. source: Fulton sample ballot source: DeKalb sample ballot source: Gwinnett sample ballot source: Cobb sample ballot source: Richmond sample ballot source: Chatham Board of Elections

  • Superior and State Court judges, all counties source: Cobb County sample ballotDecided in the nonpartisan election on May 19, 2026 (runoffs, if needed, in June). Not on the November 3 ballot.
  • Probate judges: ballot question only source: Fulton sample ballot source: Richmond sample ballot source: Atlanta News FirstIt is Proposed Constitutional Amendment 2, House Resolution 251 (Ga. L. 2026, p. 1110). Official wording on the county sample ballots: "Shall the Constitution of Georgia be amended so as to require that all probate court judges be elected in nonpartisan elections?" A yes vote requires nonpartisan elections (no party labels). A no vote keeps probate judges elected with party labels. It is a question, not a judge race. Amendment 1 is the conservation land acreage question (HR 32). Amendment 3 is the 9-1-1 fund (HR 1243). BallotReady numbered the probate question 1, which the county ballots do not support. Ballotpedia reports it as approved from unofficial results; certified results come later. The Secretary of State booklet blocked our build, so we used the county ballots instead.
