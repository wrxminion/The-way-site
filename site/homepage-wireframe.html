Homepage, shallow to deep

Locked order: Map, News, Cases, Timeline, Research. The ocean gets darker as you go down. No splash. One search, "Pull a Thread. Find its Way.", enters any depth.

1. Map: What affects me?
US state map first, working above the fold. Pick a state: ballot, representatives, elections, accountability. No intro blocking it. Keyboard and screen-reader labels on every state.
2. News: What is happening?
Three to five dated, sourced headlines with "related case" links. Shows "updated" time; if older than 48 hours it says "feed is behind" instead of looking fresh.
3. Cases: What happened, and what is established?
Cards: finding, what is unresolved, link to primary sources. Each links to its timeline.
4. Timeline: How did we get here?
Swipe rail with the Intent and Outcome pair (see /timeline/). Also embedded in each case.
5. Research: How do we know?
Two routes: Explore the evidence, Download the research (see /research/).
Everywhere: breadcrumbs, "return to the surface" link, table of contents on long pages, stable deep links, reduced-motion honored.
