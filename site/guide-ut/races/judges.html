Judges on the retention ballot

Appellate judges in Utah don't run against opponents. Voters decide whether each one stays in office (yes or no).

  • Judges do not run against anyone. Voters answer Yes or No on whether each judge stays in office ("Shall ... be retained"). source: vote.utah.gov
  • Supreme Court and Court of Appeals judges are on every ballot in the state; district, juvenile and justice court judges appear only where they serve. source: vote.utah.gov

Judges on your ballot

  • Utah Supreme Court
    Jill Pohlman source: KUER source: Deseret News source: Salt Lake Tribune    On the ballot in every county. Appointed to the Utah Supreme Court by Gov. Spencer Cox in 2022; this is her first retention vote. She is the only Supreme Court justice on the ballot: Justice Diana Hagen was also due to stand but resigned on May 8, 2026 source: KUER, May 8, 2026. Support for removal: at the Utah Republican convention in April 2026, party chair Robert Axson urged voters to vote no on her and Hagen, citing the court's unanimous July 2024 ruling that limited the Legislature's power to amend voter-approved ballot initiatives, a ruling later used to overturn the congressional map. Gov. Cox defended the effort as a proper check, saying retention elections are how voters respond to decisions they dislike. Axson told the Deseret News his objection covers all four justices who were on the court for that ruling and that Hagen and Pohlman were simply the two up this year source: Axios, Apr. 29, 2026 source: Deseret News, Apr. 30, 2026. Support for retention: the Utah State Bar urged voters to rely on the Judicial Performance Evaluation Commission (JPEC) rather than a single ruling, and said judges should not have to look over their shoulders at the Legislature or governor. JPEC found she meets or exceeds performance standards and recommended retention on a 12-0 commission vote, with survey scores of 4.8 for legal ability, 4.8 for integrity and temperament, 4.9 for administrative skills and 4.9 for procedural fairness (cumulative 19.3 out of 20) source: JPEC, Know Your Judges. The Salt Lake Tribune reports JPEC recommended retaining all of Utah's judges this year except one Weber County Justice Court judge source: Salt Lake Tribune, Oct. 12, 2026. Voters decide yes or no.
