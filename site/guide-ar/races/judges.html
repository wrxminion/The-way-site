Judges on the ballot

Arkansas holds its statewide nonpartisan judicial elections in March, not November. We found no Supreme Court or Court of Appeals seat on the November 3, 2026 ballot in a source we could verify. Three circuit judge races are in our Arkansas guide.

Ballotpedia reports Justice Nicholas Bronni won Supreme Court Position 3 outright on March 3, 2026 and lists a possible runoff date of November 3. We did not find results for a runoff. Our Arkansas guide lists three circuit judge races: District 2 Division 4, District 4 Division 2 and District 16 Division 2, each with two candidates.
