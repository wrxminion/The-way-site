Candidate · Attorney General

Robert W. Fioretti

What this job does: The state's top lawyer. The attorney general represents the state in court, enforces state laws such as consumer protection rules, takes legal action to protect the public, and works with lawmakers on new laws. A term is four years.

Republican · listed 2 of 2 on the Illinois State Board of Elections' list · on the ballot as "Robert W. Fioretti"

Occupation
Civil rights attorney
Lives in
Chicago

Background

  • Bachelor's degree from the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign; law degree from Northern Illinois University College of Law. source: en.wikipedia.org
  • Civil rights attorney and partner at Roth Fioretti LLC; earlier a senior supervising attorney in the City of Chicago's Corporation Counsel office and an assistant Illinois attorney general. source: en.wikipedia.org
  • Chicago alderman for the 2nd Ward, 2007-2015. source: wbez.org
  • Ran for Chicago mayor (2015, 2019), Cook County Board president, and Cook County state's attorney; was a Democrat until 2022 and was the Republican nominee for Cook County Board president in 2022. source: en.wikipedia.org
