Where the map comes from

District lines

U.S. Census Bureau 2024 cartographic boundary files (1:500,000): cb_2024_us_cd119_500k (Rhode Island features only) for the 2 U.S. House districts, cb_2024_44_sldu_500k for the 38 state Senate districts, cb_2024_44_sldl_500k for the 75 state House districts, cb_2024_us_county_500k for counties and cb_2024_44_place_500k for city and town limits. These show the Rhode Island General Assembly's state Senate and House districts drawn in 2022. District files are converted without simplification; county outlines and city limits are simplified for display. Near a boundary line they can be off by a block or more.

  • cb_2024_44_sldl_500k.zip: www2.census.gov, retrieved 2026-10-13
  • cb_2024_44_place_500k.zip: www2.census.gov, retrieved 2026-10-13

Census files: www2.census.gov/geo/tiger/GENZ2024/shp/.
