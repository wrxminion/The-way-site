Candidate · U.S. House, District 13

Donavan McKinney

What this job does: Your district's one vote in the U.S. House. House members write and vote on federal laws, taxes and spending, and every bill to raise taxes has to start in the House. A term is two years.

Democratic · listed 1 of 5 on the Bureau of Elections' list · on the ballot as "McKinney, Donavan"

Current office
State Representative, House District 11 (since 2025; District 14, 2023-2024)
Occupation
State Representative
Lives in
Detroit

Background

  • Bachelor's degree in public policy, University of Michigan Ford School of Public Policy (2014). source: ballotpedia.org
  • Member of the Michigan House since 2023 (District 14, then District 11 from 2025). source: ballotpedia.org
  • Former political director of SEIU Healthcare Michigan and national political director of Climate Power. source: ballotpedia.org
  • Appointed to Michigan's first Environmental Justice Council in 2020. source: ballotpedia.org
  • Attended Detroit Public Schools; first in family to graduate from college. source: donavanforcongress.com
  • Won the Aug. 4, 2026 Democratic primary over incumbent Shri Thanedar, 51.9% to 48.1%. source: michiganpublic.org
