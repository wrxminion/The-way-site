Where the map comes from

District lines

U.S. Census Bureau 2024 cartographic boundary files (1:500,000): Alaska has one at-large U.S. House seat. No congressional district file is needed. for the 1 U.S. House districts, cb_2024_02_sldu_500k for the 20 state Senate districts, cb_2024_02_sldl_500k for the 40 state House districts, cb_2024_us_county_500k for counties and cb_2024_02_place_500k for city and town limits. These show the the 2024 state Senate and House district boundaries for Alaska. District files are converted without simplification; county outlines and city limits are simplified for display. Near a boundary line they can be off by a block or more.

Files

  • cb_2024_02_sldu_500k.zip: www2.census.gov, retrieved 2026-10-13
  • cb_2024_02_sldl_500k.zip: www2.census.gov, retrieved 2026-10-13
  • cb_2024_02_place_500k.zip: www2.census.gov, retrieved 2026-10-13
  • ak.csv: data.openstates.org, retrieved 2026-10-13

Census files: www2.census.gov/geo/tiger/GENZ2024/shp/.
