Local measure · Baltimore County

Baltimore County Question FCounty Ordinance – Refuse Disposal Borrowing

What your vote means

YES

A YES vote lets Baltimore County borrow $5,000,000 for refuse disposal projects, such as building, extending, repairing, closing and capping county refuse facilities.

NO

A NO vote does not allow the county to borrow $5,000,000 for this purpose.

Our one-line summary of the official wording.

In plain words

Our summary of the official materials and local reporting.

  • Bill 42-26 asks voters to let Baltimore County borrow $5,000,000 for refuse disposal projects, such as building, extending, repairing, closing and capping county refuse facilities. source: elections.maryland.gov
  • The Baltimore County Charter requires that borrowing ordinances be approved by voters; each question limits borrowing to its stated amount and purpose. source: baltimorecountymd.gov
  • Bond money is used only after the County Executive proposes and the County Council approves the bonds in the capital budget. source: baltimorecountymd.gov

Cost: $5,000,000 in borrowing. The county repays the bonds with interest from its yearly General Fund budget, generally over about 20 years (county borrowing brochure; the county's debt payments were 9.4% of General Fund revenue in 2023). Total cost with interest was not found for this question.

Why people want it, and why others object

Why this is on the ballot

  • The county ballot asks voters to approve borrowing under Sections 705(a) and 718 of the Baltimore County Charter (Bill 42-26). Baltimore County puts borrowing questions on the ballot at each general election; the 2024 ballot's questions covered about $578.6 million. source: baltimorecountymd.gov
  • Baltimore County's bonds are rated AAA by Moody's, S&P and Fitch, according to the county. source: baltimorecountymd.gov

What you will see on your ballot

Official ballot wording, word for word (tap to open)

Word for word from the State Board of Elections' official Baltimore ballot: elections.maryland.gov ↗.

County Ordinance Refuse Disposal Borrowing
(Bill 42-26) AN ORDINANCE to authorize and empower Baltimore County, Maryland to borrow $5,000,000 for refuse disposal projects, including but not limited to: construction, reconstruction, extension, acquisition, improvement, enlargement, alteration, closing and capping, repair, and modernization of county refuse disposal facilities, to be undertaken pursuant to Sections 705(a) and 718 of the Baltimore County Charter. A vote FOR the bond issue authorizes Baltimore County to borrow $5,000,000 for refuse disposal projects. A vote AGAINST the bond issue precludes Baltimore County from borrowing $5,000,000 for refuse disposal projects.
