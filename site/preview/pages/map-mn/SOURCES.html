Where the map comes from

District lines

U.S. Census Bureau 2024 cartographic boundary files (1:500,000): cb_2024_us_cd119_500k (Minnesota features only) for the 8 U.S. House districts, cb_2024_27_sldu_500k for the 67 state Senate districts, cb_2024_27_sldl_500k for the 134 state House districts, cb_2024_us_county_500k for counties and cb_2024_27_place_500k for city and town limits. These show the Minnesota's 2022 state Senate and House districts, drawn by a court panel after the 2020 census. District files are converted without simplification; county outlines and city limits are simplified for display. Near a boundary line they can be off by a block or more.
