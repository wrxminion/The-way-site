Judges on the ballot

In Wyoming, the Secretary of State's Voter's Guide lists "Judicial Retentions" among the offices up this year. The candidate roster lists these judges by court with no party, and each name appears once. The exact yes or no question printed on the ballot is not in the files we have, so check your sample ballot.

What each court does

  • Wyoming Supreme Court: Justices serve eight-year terms; the Voter's Guide lists "Judicial Retentions" among the offices up this year. source: sos.wyo.gov (Wyoming Secretary of State)
  • Chancery Court: Judges serve six-year terms. source: sos.wyo.gov (Wyoming Secretary of State)
  • District Court: Judges serve six-year terms. source: sos.wyo.gov (Wyoming Secretary of State)
  • Circuit Court: Judges serve four-year terms. source: sos.wyo.gov (Wyoming Secretary of State)
