INCOMPLETE. What this guide covers and what it does not. The 86 races and 130 candidates come from the Secretary of State’s candidate roster ↗, printed September 9, 2026. Anything that changed after that date (a withdrawal, a write-in, a late filing) is not checked here. Candidate background is thin: only 16 of 130 candidates (statewide and U.S. races) have a profile with background, and that came from the WyoFile 2026 election guide, the candidates’ own answers, not re-checked. All other candidates, including every State Senate and State House candidate, show “We have not yet found background information.” County, city, school board and community college races are not in this guide. The office descriptions say only what the Secretary of State’s files say (the term length); the files do not describe the duties of the governor, auditor, treasurer or superintendent.

Statewide offices, Congress and courts

  • U.S. Senate2 candidates

    Senators serve six-year terms; one of Wyoming's two seats is on the ballot this year.

  • Governor3 candidates

    A four-year term; one of five statewide offices on this year's ballot (governor, secretary of state, state auditor, state treasurer and superintendent of public instruction).

  • Secretary of State3 candidates

    A four-year term; the Secretary of State's office includes the Elections Division, which publishes the candidate roster, the election calendar and the Voter's Guide that this guide uses.

  • State Auditor1 candidate

    A four-year term; the Secretary of State's file lists this office but does not describe what it does.

  • State Treasurer1 candidate

    A four-year term; the Secretary of State's file lists this office but does not describe what it does.

  • Superintendent of Public Instruction2 candidates

    A four-year term; the Secretary of State's file lists this office but does not describe what it does.

  • U.S. House (one seat for the whole state)4 candidates

    Representatives serve two-year terms; Wyoming has one seat, chosen by voters across the whole state.

On the Wyoming ballot this year, voters choose a U.S. senator, a U.S. representative, a governor, a secretary of state, a state auditor, a state treasurer and a superintendent of public instruction. The State Auditor and State Treasurer races each have one candidate on the roster. Judges are on the ballot too (see the judges page).
