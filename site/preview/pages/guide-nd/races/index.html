Every race, every candidate

All 148 candidates on the Secretary of State's official list for federal, statewide and legislative offices, each with a profile page. County and local races are on your sample ballot.

Not in this guide: North Dakota District Court judge races (Nos. 1 to 11), county offices (commissioner, sheriff, State's Attorney, auditor, recorder, treasurer, clerk of court, official newspaper), soil conservation district supervisors and Garrison Diversion directors. They are on the Secretary of State's list but we did not build pages for them. Check your sample ballot. Even-numbered legislative districts are not up this year, except the special House races in Districts 20, 26 and 42.

    North Dakota's one vote in the U.S.

The Secretary of State's 2026 General Election candidate list has no Governor or U.S. Senate contest. It does list U.S. Representative, Secretary of State, Attorney General, Agriculture Commissioner, Tax Commissioner, Superintendent of Public Instruction (an unexpired 2-year term), two Public Service Commissioner races and two Supreme Court races. Names and parties are as printed on that list. The list does not mark write-ins or withdrawals.

U.S. House

North Dakota's one vote in the U.S. House. House members write and vote on federal laws, taxes and spending. Every tax bill has to start in the House. A term is two years.
