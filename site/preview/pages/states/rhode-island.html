Voting in Rhode Island

General election: Tuesday, November 3, 2026.

At a glance

Register
Online, by mail or in person

Many people are registered automatically when they deal with the DMV or certain state agencies. You must register before the deadline below.

ID at the polls
Photo ID asked for

Without photo ID, vote a provisional ballot that counts if your signature matches. No ID? Ask your election office whether it offers a free one for voting. First-time voters who registered by mail may need to show ID under federal law.

Mail voting
Anyone can vote by mail

Return deadline: received by 8 p.m. Nov 3 (close of polls).

Early in-person voting
Yes

Oct 14 - Nov 2 (20 days before)

After a conviction
Voting rights return when you leave prison

Only people in prison for a felony lose the vote; restored on release.

Where the road gets harder

Every rule is a cost, and it doesn't fall on everyone the same. A deadline, a document or a trip to an office is easy for some people and a real obstacle for others: people who work hourly jobs, move often, live far from an office, don't drive, or have a disability. Here is what that looks like in Rhode Island.

  • Registration closes before Election Day (in person: Sunday, October 4). Miss it and you can't vote in this election. If you've moved, your registration may need updating by then too.
  • Mail ballots have to arrive by Election Day; a postmark isn't enough. A ballot mailed in the last days can arrive too late and won't count, so return it in person if it's close.
