Statewide, South Dakota voters choose a governor and lieutenant governor (as one ticket), a U.S. senator, the U.S. representative for the whole state, attorney general, secretary of state, state auditor, state treasurer, commissioner of school and public lands, and a public utilities commissioner. Four constitutional amendments are also on the ballot. The official candidate list has no judge rows, so we do not know whether a judge retention question is on your ballot. Check your sample ballot.

U.S. House

South Dakota's one vote in the U.S. House, chosen by the whole state. House members write and vote on federal laws, taxes and spending, and every bill to raise taxes has to start in the House. A term is two years.
