What this job does

One of 65 state representatives in the Colorado General Assembly, which writes state laws and passes the yearly state budget (the "Long Bill") in a session of up to 120 days. A term is two years, four in a row at most. source: en.wikipedia.org

Background

  • Spent about two decades at KOA radio, including as political sales manager for Clear Channel's Denver-market stations. source: ballotpedia.org
  • Served on the Castle Rock Town Council and formerly chaired the Castle Rock Planning Commission. source: ballotpedia.org
  • Attended the University of Alabama. source: ballotpedia.org
  • Serves on the Business Affairs and Labor, Finance, and Transportation, Housing and Local Government committees. source: theballotbrief.com
