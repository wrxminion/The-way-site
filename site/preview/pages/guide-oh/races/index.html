Statewide, Ohio voters choose a governor and lieutenant governor (as one team), attorney general, auditor of state, secretary of state and treasurer of state, plus a U.S. senator in a special election and two Ohio Supreme Court justices. Supreme Court candidates are listed with their party on the ballot. U.S. House candidates are listed only for Districts 1 and 8, from the Hamilton County list. The other 13 U.S. House districts are missing.

U.S. House

Your district's one vote in the U.S. House. House members write and vote on federal laws, taxes and spending, and every bill to raise taxes has to start in the House. A term is two years.

State Senate

Your district's vote in the Ohio Senate. Senators write and vote on state laws and the state budget, approve many of the governor's appointments, and can override the governor's veto with three-fifths of the vote. A term is four years.

  • District 72 candidates
  • District 92 candidates

17 of the 33 Ohio Senate seats (the odd-numbered districts) are on the ballot; senators serve four-year terms. We only have the Hamilton County Board of Elections list, so only Districts 7 and 9 are shown. Other counties are not shown yet.

State House
