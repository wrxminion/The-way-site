Candidate · State House, District 58

Carl Newton

What this job does: Your district's vote in the Oklahoma House of Representatives. Members write and vote on state laws and the state budget; bills to raise taxes start in the House. A term is two years.

Republican · listed 1 of 2 on the State Election Board's list · on the ballot as "CARL NEWTON"

Background

  • Earned a bachelor's degree from Oklahoma State University and a Doctor of Optometry from Northeastern State University. source: okhouse.gov
  • Practiced optometry near Cherokee for many years before selling the eye clinic; also owns a family farm. source: okhouse.gov
  • Won House District 58 in 2016, succeeding Jeff Hickman, and took office in November 2016. source: enidnews.com
  • Chairs the House Health and Human Services Oversight Committee. source: okhouse.gov
  • Co-authored the Groundwater Modernization Act (SB 259). source: okhouse.gov
  • Born in Waynoka, Oklahoma. source: okhouse.gov
