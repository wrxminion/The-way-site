Texas voters choose a U.S. senator, a governor, a lieutenant governor and five other statewide officers this year: attorney general, comptroller, land commissioner, agriculture commissioner and one railroad commissioner. They also choose four Texas Supreme Court justices, three Court of Criminal Appeals judges and three justices of the 15th Court of Appeals, a statewide appeals court. Judges in Texas run in partisan races, like other offices. Texas no longer has straight-ticket voting, so you mark each race.
