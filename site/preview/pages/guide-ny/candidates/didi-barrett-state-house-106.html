Didi Barrett

What this job does: Your district's vote in the New York State Assembly (150 members), the larger house of the Legislature. Members write and vote on state laws and the state budget. A term is two years.

Background

  • Elected in a special election in March 2012; first woman and first Democrat to represent the district source: assembly.ny.gov
  • Before elected office, worked as a writer, museum professional and not-for-profit leader; founding chair of Girls Incorporated of NYC source: assembly.ny.gov
  • Won the Democratic primary held June 23, 2026 (per WAMC headline); faced challenger Sam Hodge source: wamc.org
