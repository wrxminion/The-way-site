Michael Novakhov

What this job does: Your district's vote in the New York State Assembly (150 members), the larger house of the Legislature. Members write and vote on state laws and the state budget. A term is two years.

Republican / Conservative · listed 2 of 2 on the State Board of Elections' list · on the ballot as "Michael Novakhov"

Background

  • About 20 years in the radio business; co-founded Freedom FM in 2019 source: assembly.ny.gov
  • Born in the former USSR and immigrated to the United States with family source: assembly.ny.gov
  • Elected to the Assembly November 8, 2022, defeating incumbent Steven Cymbrowitz source: ballotpedia.org
  • Won re-election in 2024 by 232 votes over Joey Cohen-Saban source: ballotpedia.org
