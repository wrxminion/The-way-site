Local measure · Logan County

Logan County QuestionLogan County schools: 5.9-cent property tax for school buildings

What your vote means

YES

A FOR vote lets the Logan County school board levy a 5.9-cent tax on every $100 of taxed real and personal property to pay for school buildings.

NO

An AGAINST vote cancels the 5.9-cent tax, so the school building money it would raise is not collected.

Our one-line summary of the official wording.

In plain words

Our summary of the official materials and local reporting.

  • The rate is 5.9 cents per $100 of taxed value. Baird Financial estimates it raises about $1.15 million a year locally plus about $950,000 a year from the state, about $2.1 million together. source: franklinfavorite.com
  • The district's facilities plan lists $61,346,627 of building needs through 2030, the largest being Auburn School at $32,949,496. source: franklinfavorite.com
  • Community members filed a petition against the tax and gathered enough signatures to force a public vote. source: wbko.com

Cost: The district's estimate: about 56% of taxpayers would pay less than $1 more a week. It would raise about $1.15 million a year locally plus about $950,000 a year in state matching money.

Why people want it, and why others object

Why this is on the ballot

  • The school board first approved the levy in June 2025 and voted in August 2025 to put it on the November 2026 ballot. Auburn has added about 30 students a year for four years, according to the superintendent. source: wbko.com

Why supporters want it

  • Superintendent Dan Costellow: "This is important for our students to have safe, adequate, and up-to-date facilities." He said that if it fails, the district would have to redistrict Auburn students to avoid overcrowding. source: wbko.com
