Where the map comes from

District lines

U.S. Census Bureau 2024 cartographic boundary files (1:500,000): cb_2024_us_cd119_500k (New Hampshire features only) for the 2 U.S. House districts, cb_2024_33_sldu_500k for the 24 state Senate districts, cb_2024_33_sldl_500k for the 400 state House districts, cb_2024_us_county_500k for counties and cb_2024_33_place_500k for city and town limits. These show the New Hampshire state legislative districts (the 164 county districts in the file; floterial districts are not in it). District files are converted without simplification; county outlines and city limits are simplified for display. Near a boundary line they can be off by a block or more.

Files

  • cb_2024_33_sldu_500k.zip: www2.census.gov, retrieved 2026-10-13
  • cb_2024_33_sldl_500k.zip: www2.census.gov, retrieved 2026-10-13
  • cb_2024_33_place_500k.zip: www2.census.gov, retrieved 2026-10-13
  • nh.csv: data.openstates.org, retrieved 2026-10-13

Census files: www2.census.gov/geo/tiger/GENZ2024/shp/.
