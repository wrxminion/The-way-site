Statewide races

  • Governor3 candidates

    The Governor is the head of Nevada's state government for a 4-year term and can serve 2 terms.

  • Lieutenant Governor3 candidates

    The Lieutenant Governor leads the state Senate and can break tie votes there.

  • Secretary of State4 candidates

    The Secretary of State is Nevada's chief election officer for a 4-year term.

  • Attorney General2 candidates

    The Attorney General is Nevada's top lawyer for a 4-year term.

  • State Treasurer2 candidates

    The State Treasurer manages and invests the state's money for a 4-year term.

  • Controller2 candidates

    The State Controller is Nevada's chief fiscal officer for a 4-year term.

  • Supreme Court, Seat B1 candidate
  • Supreme Court, Seat D1 candidate

Every Nevada ballot lists Governor, Lieutenant Governor, Secretary of State, Attorney General, Treasurer, Controller, and 2 Nevada Supreme Court seats (B and D). Each of these races includes a "None of these candidates" choice. There is no U.S. Senate race in Nevada this year.

U.S. House

A U.S. Representative speaks for one Nevada district in Congress for a 2-year term. Representatives vote on federal laws, taxes, and spending. Nevada has 4 districts; District 2 is not shown (no candidate list found).
