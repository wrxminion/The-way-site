question 6Abortion rights in the state constitution

Constitutional amendment (initiative, second vote): State Question No. 6: Amendment to the Nevada Constitution (Initiative Petition C-05-2023), placed on the ballot by voters who signed petitions (State Question No. 6: Amendment to the Nevada Constitution (Initiative Petition C-05-2023)). Passes with Majority YES. This is the second and final vote: it passed in 2024 and must pass again in 2026 to become part of the Nevada Constitution..

Read the full text of the measure: Ballot Questions booklet ↗

In plain words

Nevada law already allows abortion up to 24 weeks, and later to protect the patient's life or health. Question 6 would also put an abortion right in the state constitution. A constitution is harder to change than a regular law. (clarkcountynv.gov ↗)

Biggest change

  • Creates a constitutional right to abortion from the start of pregnancy up to "fetal viability." After that point, the right still applies when care is needed to protect the pregnant person's life or health. source: clarkcountynv.gov
  • State or local government could limit this right only if it has a "compelling state interest" and uses the least restrictive way to meet it. source: clarkcountynv.gov
  • The abortion must be done by a qualified healthcare professional. source: clarkcountynv.gov
  • If approved in both 2024 and 2026, it takes effect on Nov 24, 2026, when the Nevada Supreme Court canvasses the vote. source: clarkcountynv.gov

Who it affects most: People who are or may become pregnant, healthcare professionals who provide abortions, and state and local lawmakers.

Cost to the state: The Legislative Counsel Bureau's Fiscal Analysis Division says the financial effect on state or local governments "cannot be determined with any reasonable degree of certainty."

What your vote means

YES

A YES vote adds a right to abortion to the Nevada Constitution. It would take effect when the vote is made official in late Nov 2026.

NO

A NO vote leaves the Nevada Constitution unchanged. Abortion stays legal under current state law, which allows it up to 24 weeks and later to protect the patient's life or health.

Our one-line summary. Nevada does not publish an official "what your vote means" statement; the ballot title above is the official description.

Why people want it, and why others object

Why this is on the ballot

Voters put this on the ballot by signing a petition. It changes the state constitution, so only another statewide vote can change it back. It passes with Majority YES. This is the second and final vote: it passed in 2024 and must pass again in 2026 to become part of the Nevada Constitution..

  • Citizen initiative petition (C-05-2023), led by Nevadans for Reproductive Freedom. Voters approved it the first time in Nov 2024. source: clarkcountynv.gov
  • Voters approved Question 6 the first time in Nov 2024 by nearly 29 percentage points. A constitutional initiative must pass at two general elections in a row. source: news3lv.com
  • Abortion has been legal in Nevada under a state law approved by voters in 1990. That law allows abortion within 24 weeks of pregnancy. source: clarkcountynv.gov
  • Question 6 and Question 7 are the only statewide questions on Nevada's 2026 ballot, the fewest since 2012. source: news3lv.com
