Candidate · State House, District 25

Lorenza M. Wilkins

What this job does: Your district's vote in the N.C. House of Representatives. Members write and vote on state laws and the state budget, and propose constitutional amendments like the ones on this ballot. A term is two years.

Democrat · listed 2 of 3 on the State Board of Elections' list · on the ballot as "Lorenza M. Wilkins"

Occupation
Nonprofit executive (chief administrative officer, Inter-Faith Food Shuttle)
Lives in
Battleboro

Background

  • Chief administrative officer at the Inter-Faith Food Shuttle. source: rockymounttelegram.com
  • Previously worked for CSX, starting as a conductor in Rocky Mount, and for IBM and Motorola in Research Triangle Park. source: rockymounttelegram.com
  • Bachelor's, master's and doctoral degrees from Columbia Southern University; attended N.C. Central University on an athletic scholarship. source: rockymounttelegram.com
  • Native of the Lake Gaston area; has lived in Nash County for more than 27 years. source: lorenzawilkinsfornc.com
  • Lost the 2024 House District 25 race to Allen Chesser by 461 votes; won the March 2026 Democratic primary with about 57%. source: rockymounttelegram.com
