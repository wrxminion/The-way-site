The record

What did we believe? What actually happened?

Descend into the cases ↓

More than two dozen moments in American history, studied one at a time from the original record: the laws, the court opinions, the reports, and the people who were there.

Each case lays out what happened, what it shows, the strongest objections and our answers to them, and what would change our minds. Every claim links to its source. These are preliminary studies, not final verdicts, and every correction stays on the record with its date.

The first cases ask one question: when Americans were shut out of the vote, the jury box or the courthouse, what happened to them?
