Dozens of moments in American history, studied one at a time from the original record: the laws, the court opinions, the reports, and the people who were there.

These are preliminary studies, not final verdicts. A "Sources checked" date records source work. It does not by itself identify a human reviewer or establish independent review. Read each page's limitations.

The cases examine what happened, the evidence, objections, and what would change the conclusions. Source links, claim tables, human review records and dated correction histories are still being completed. The research methodology working draft sets out the proposed standard. It does not establish that every case meets it.

The first cases ask one question: when Americans were shut out of the vote, the jury box or the courthouse, what happened to them?
