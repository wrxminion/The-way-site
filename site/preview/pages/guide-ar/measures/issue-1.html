issue 1Citizens-only voting

Legislatively referred constitutional amendment: Issue 1 (HJR 1018 of 2025), placed on the ballot by the Legislature as Issue 1 (HJR 1018 of 2025) ↗. Passes with A majority of the votes cast on the issue..

Read the full text of the measure: official text ↗

In plain words

Arkansas already requires voters to be U.S. citizens. This amendment restates that rule in stronger words and says it applies to state and local elections, so a city could not let non-citizens vote. (uaex.uada.edu ↗)

Biggest change

  • Adds to Article 3, sec. 1 that only a U.S. citizen who meets the voter qualifications may vote in Arkansas. source: arkleg.state.ar.us
  • Says a person who does not meet the voter qualifications may not vote in any state or local election in Arkansas. source: arkleg.state.ar.us
  • The four current voter qualifications stay the same. source: uaex.uada.edu
  • Takes effect Jan. 1, 2027. source: arkleg.state.ar.us

Who it affects most: People who are not U.S. citizens and cities or towns; the guide notes no Arkansas community is reported to be planning non-citizen voting.

Cost to the state: No state cost estimate was found in the official text or the UA Extension guide.

What your vote means

YES

A YES (FOR) vote adds citizens-only voting language to Article 3 of the Arkansas Constitution, starting Jan. 1, 2027.

NO

A NO (AGAINST) vote keeps the current wording of Article 3, which already lists U.S. citizenship as a voter qualification.

Our one-line summary. Arkansas does not publish an official "what your vote means" statement; the ballot title above is the official description.

Why people want it, and why others object

Why this is on the ballot

The Legislature put this on the ballot. It changes the state constitution, so only another statewide vote can change it back. It passes with A majority of the votes cast on the issue..

  • Arkansas General Assembly (HJR 1018, 2025 regular session). Sponsors: Rep. David Ray and Sen. John Payton. source: arkleg.state.ar.us
  • Passed by the legislature in April 2025 as HJR 1018. source: uaex.uada.edu
  • About 117,000 of Arkansas's 3.1 million residents are not U.S. citizens (2024 American Community Survey, as cited by UA Extension). source: uaex.uada.edu
  • A Talk Business & Politics/Hendrix College poll in August 2026 found 60% for, 27% against, 13% undecided. source: kuaf.com

What you will see on your ballot

Official ballot wording, word for word (tap to open)

The official ballot title, word for word from the Arkansas General Assembly ↗.

AN AMENDMENT TO THE ARKANSAS CONSTITUTION TO CREATE "THE CITIZENS ONLY VOTING AMENDMENT"; TO PROVIDE THAT ONLY A CITIZEN OF THE UNITED STATES MEETING THE QUALIFICATIONS OF AN ELECTOR UNDER THE ARKANSAS CONSTITUTION MAY VOTE IN AN ELECTION IN THIS STATE; AND PROVIDING THAT A PERSON WHO DOES NOT MEET THE QUALIFICATIONS OF AN ELECTOR UNDER THE ARKANSAS CONSTITUTION SHALL NOT BE PERMITTED TO VOTE IN ANY STATE OR LOCAL ELECTION HELD IN THIS STATE.

FOR
AGAINST
