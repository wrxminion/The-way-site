Candidate · State House, District 39 (Positions 1 and 2, two separate contests)

Kathryn Lewandowsky

What this job does: Your district has two seats in the Washington House of Representatives (98 members), each its own contest. Representatives write and vote on state laws and the budget. A term is two years.

Position 1 · Prefers Democratic Party · listed 1 of 4 on the Secretary of State's list · on the ballot as "Kathryn Lewandowsky"

Occupation
Registered nurse

Background

  • Registered nurse since 1991 (LPN since 1987), with experience in oncology, ICU and hospice. source: sos.wa.gov (Secretary of State)
  • Washington State Nurses Association local unit chair and grievance officer at St. Joseph's Hospital in the 1990s. source: sos.wa.gov (Secretary of State)
  • B.S.N., Western Washington University (2017); Master's in Legal Studies, Purdue Global University (2024). source: sos.wa.gov (Secretary of State)
  • Vice chair of the Whole Washington board and treasurer of the One Payer States board since 2019. source: sos.wa.gov (Secretary of State)
