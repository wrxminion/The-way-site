Every race, every candidate

All 304 candidates on the Secretary of the Commonwealth's official list for federal, statewide and legislative offices, each with a profile page. County and local races are on your sample ballot.

Statewide and U.S. Senate

  • Governor and Lieutenant Governor3 candidates

    The governor runs the state government, proposes the budget, signs or vetoes bills and nominates judges.

  • Attorney General2 candidates

    The state's top lawyer.

  • Secretary of the Commonwealth2 candidates

    Runs state elections and keeps public records, business filings and the state archives.

  • Treasurer and Receiver General2 candidates

    Manages the state's money and investments, runs the state Lottery and holds unclaimed property.

  • State Auditor2 candidates

    Checks how state agencies spend money and whether programs work.

  • U.S. Senate4 candidates

    One of two people who speak for Massachusetts in the U.S.

The governor and lieutenant governor run together as one ticket. All 40 state Senate seats, all 160 state House seats and all 8 Governor's Council seats are also on the ballot.
