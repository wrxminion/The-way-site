Judges on the ballot

Nevada votes on two Nevada Supreme Court seats this year, Seat B and Seat D. Each has one candidate, a sitting justice, and each race includes a "None of these candidates" choice. Supreme Court races are nonpartisan.

Both names are confirmed on the Nevada Secretary of State's certified candidate list (version dated August 25, 2026) and on the Clark County contest list. See the section below for district, justice and municipal court races. source: NV Secretary of State list

Supreme Court, Seat B

  • Kristina Pickering source: Clark County NV, 26G noticeIncumbent, listed as nonpartisan. The county notice shows one candidate for the seat and our Nevada guide counts one candidate.

Supreme Court, Seat D

  • Douglas Herndon source: Clark County NV, 26G noticeIncumbent, listed as nonpartisan. The county notice shows one candidate for the seat and our Nevada guide counts one candidate.

County, district and local judges

What this section covers. Every judicial office on the Nevada Secretary of State's "2026 General Election Statewide Certified List of Candidates", the version dated August 25, 2026. That list includes filings from all counties. We read it directly. For Clark County we also checked the county's own contest list dated August 24, 2026.

What it does not cover. Constables, which are not judges. Any candidate change after August 25, 2026. Incumbency and background of each candidate. Nevada justice and municipal courts that are not on the state list, for example any Sparks Municipal Court or Reno-Verdi and Sparks Justice Court seat. We saw none of those on the list, but we did not check each city or county separately.

Court of Appeals. The state list has no Nevada Court of Appeals seat, so none appears to be up in 2026.

How the contests work. All Nevada judicial races are nonpartisan. A seat with one name is an uncontested race. For the Supreme Court and district court the county notice also shows a "None of these candidates" choice.
