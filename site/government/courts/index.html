Layer 1 / How a case moves

Three levels, from trial to the top.

District courts are the trial courts. The courts of appeals sit above them, and the Supreme Court sits above the courts of appeals.

  1. 94District courtsThe trial courts, where most federal cases start.
  2. 13Courts of appealsHear appeals from the district courts. See the circuit map
  3. 1Supreme CourtThe highest court. Meet the nine justices
Who chooses federal judges, and for how long?

The president nominates judges for the Supreme Court, the courts of appeals and the district courts, and the Senate confirms or rejects them. These judges hold their offices during good behavior, so they have no fixed term. Congress creates appeals and district judgeships by law.

When was it built?

The cornerstone was laid on October 13, 1932. The building was completed in 1935, when the Court was finally able to occupy its own building.

Source: Supreme Court building history ↗
Who designed it?

Architect Cass Gilbert, at the request of Chief Justice William Howard Taft. A classical Corinthian style was chosen.
