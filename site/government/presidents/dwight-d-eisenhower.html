One thing to research

The Interstate Highway System

Signed June 29, 1956.

The Federal-Aid Highway Act of 1956 set up a 41,000-mile system of interstate highways and authorized $25 billion for fiscal years 1957 to 1969.

Supporters said

The law called the system important to national defense. It boosted the economy and made long drives faster and safer.

Critics said

Highways through cities cut through neighborhoods and destroyed housing, much of it in poorer areas, and helped empty out downtowns. Many small towns were bypassed.
