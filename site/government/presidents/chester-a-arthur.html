One thing to research

The Pendleton Civil Service Act

Signed January 16, 1883.

The law began hiring by competitive exam for many federal jobs, created the Civil Service Commission, and barred firing covered workers for political reasons.

Supporters said

Arthur had become a strong reformer after Garfield's assassination. Republican losses in the 1882 elections also pushed him to sign.

Critics said

A Democratic senator called it a bill to keep Republicans in their jobs. It did not cover people already hired, most federal workers, or any state and city workers.
