In History.

One thing to research

The Sixteenth Amendment (income tax)

Passed by Congress July 2, 1909. Ratified February 3, 1913.

The amendment lets Congress tax incomes "from whatever source derived" without dividing the tax among the states by population.

Supporters said

Farm groups, Populists, Democrats under William Jennings Bryan and progressive Republicans had long wanted an income tax. Taft pushed the amendment through.

A twist

The National Archives says conservatives proposed the amendment "hoping to kill the idea for good."
