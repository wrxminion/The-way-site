One thing to research

The Immigration Act of 1924

1924.

The law limited each nationality to 2 percent of its number in the 1890 census, which cut immigration from southern and eastern Europe, and it shut out immigrants from Asia.

Supporters said

Supporters of limits wanted to preserve what the State Department historian calls "the ideal of U.S. homogeneity."

Critics said

Many in Japan were deeply offended and the Japanese government protested. Others in the United States pushed to raise the quotas.
