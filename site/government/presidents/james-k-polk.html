One thing to research

The Mexican-American War

1846 to 1848. The peace treaty was signed February 2, 1848.

In the Treaty of Guadalupe Hidalgo, Mexico gave up land that includes present-day California, Nevada, Utah and New Mexico and accepted the Rio Grande as the Texas border. The United States paid $15 million. The Senate approved the treaty March 10, 1848.

Supporters said

Polk wanted California and a nation reaching the Pacific. Expansionists in his own party pushed for every possible gain, and some called for taking all of Mexico.

Critics said

Most Whigs opposed the war, and Congressman Abraham Lincoln called it unconstitutional. Many opponents saw it as an attempt to spread slavery into new territory.
