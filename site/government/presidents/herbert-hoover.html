The budget years: fiscal 1930 to 1933.

Congress writes and passes the laws that set taxes and spending. The president can sign or veto them, and today also sends Congress a budget proposal each year. Here a fiscal year counts toward a presidency when it began while that president was in office. Wars, recessions and laws passed in earlier years also shape these numbers, so they show what happened during the years, not who caused it. Dollars are not adjusted for inflation.
