One thing to research

The Spanish-American War

1898. The Senate approved the peace treaty February 6, 1899.

Congress passed a war resolution April 20, 1898. In the Treaty of Paris, Spain gave up Cuba and gave the United States Puerto Rico, Guam and the Philippines. The Senate approved it by one vote.

Supporters said

Many Americans were angry after the sinking of the battleship Maine ("Remember the Maine!"), and businesses with interests on the island worried about their investments.

Critics said

Anti-imperialists, among them William Jennings Bryan, Mark Twain, Andrew Carnegie and former President Benjamin Harrison, said overseas expansion would cost too much and break with the country's isolationism.
