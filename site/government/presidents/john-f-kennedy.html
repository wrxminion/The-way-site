One thing to research

The Cuban Missile Crisis

October 1962.

A U-2 spy plane photographed Soviet missile sites in Cuba on October 14. Kennedy ordered a naval "quarantine" on October 22, and on October 28 Soviet leader Nikita Khrushchev said the missiles would be taken out.

The choice he made

Kennedy chose the quarantine, a word picked to set it apart legally from a blockade.

The other options

The Joint Chiefs of Staff and others argued for an air strike followed by an invasion. Part of the deal, removing U.S. missiles from Turkey, was kept secret.
