One thing to research

The Pullman Strike

1894, in his second presidency.

About 150,000 railroad workers struck in support of workers at the Pullman railcar company town outside Chicago. Cleveland sent federal troops, over the objection of Illinois Governor John Altgeld.

How people saw it

Many observers wondered whether the nation was on the brink of "either anarchy or presidential tyranny."

What followed

His handling of the strike turned many Northern workers away from his Democratic Party.
