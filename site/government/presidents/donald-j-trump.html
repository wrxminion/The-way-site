One thing to research

The 2017 tax cut (Tax Cuts and Jobs Act)

Signed December 22, 2017, in his first presidency.

The law cut the top corporate tax rate to a flat 21 percent, nearly doubled the standard deduction, capped the state and local tax deduction at $10,000, and set the Affordable Care Act's coverage penalty to $0 from 2019. Final votes on December 20, 2017: Senate 51 to 48, House 224 to 201. The Congressional Budget Office estimated it would add about $1.46 trillion to deficits over 10 years, before any economic growth effects.

Supporters said

Supporters said it would bring strong economic benefits and simplify the tax code. Studies found corporate investment rose by about 11 percent.

Critics said

Studies found it added to the federal debt and raised after-tax incomes most for the wealthy.
