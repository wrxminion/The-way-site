Governor, other statewide offices and U.S. Senate

  • Governor5 candidates

    Leads the executive branch: enforces state laws, signs or vetoes bills, and appoints agency heads and board chairs.

  • Lieutenant Governor2 candidates

    Second in the executive branch; becomes governor if that office is vacant.

  • Attorney General2 candidates

    The state's chief law enforcement officer: enforces state laws, prosecutes felony cases, and is the lawyer for state agencies.

  • Secretary of State1 candidate

    The state's chief elections official.

  • General Treasurer2 candidates

    The state's chief investment officer: manages public funds and state debt, runs the retirement and pension funds, and returns unclaimed property.

  • U.S. Senate3 candidates

    One of Rhode Island's two U.S.

Statewide, Rhode Island voters choose a governor, lieutenant governor, attorney general, secretary of state, general treasurer and one U.S. senator.
