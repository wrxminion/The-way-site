Candidate · U.S. Senate

Michael Anthony Bahry

What this job does: One of Rhode Island's two U.S. senators. Senators write and vote on laws and approve or reject judicial and cabinet nominations. A term is six years.

Independent · listed 3 of 3 on the Rhode Island Department of State's list · on the ballot as "MICHAEL ANTHONY BAHRY"

Occupation

Lives in
East Providence

Background

  • Teaches theology at Salve Regina University for about three years. source: whatsupnewp.com
