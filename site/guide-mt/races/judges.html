Judges on the ballot

One Montana Supreme Court seat (Justice #4) is on the ballot, with two candidates. Judges run without a party label. District court judges are also on some local ballots; this page covers only the Supreme Court seat.

Names, parties and ballot order are as recorded in our candidate profiles, compiled on October 15, 2026 from the Secretary of State's list. Background lines come from the pages linked on each line. A source marked "(unreviewed)" was not independently reviewed. It links to a news or reference page, not an official one. Candidate order: Eddy is listed first, Wilson second.

Montana Supreme Court, Justice #4

  • Amy Eddy source: Montana Secretary of StateNow: District judge, Montana 11th Judicial District (Flathead County), appointed 2015. Appointed to the 11th Judicial District Court on October 22, 2015 by Gov. Steve Bullock; ran unopposed in 2016 and won retention with 81.32% of the vote. source: ballotpedia.org (unreviewed) B.A., California Polytechnic State University; J.D., University of Montana. source: ballotpedia.org (unreviewed) About 15 years in private practice, mainly civil litigation, before joining the bench. source: kxlf.com (unreviewed) Won the June 2, 2026 primary with 51.40% of the vote (140,131 votes). source: en.wikipedia.org (unreviewed) Full profile.
  • Dan Wilson source: Montana Secretary of StateNow: District judge, Montana 11th Judicial District (Flathead County); elected 2016. Elected to the 11th Judicial District Court in 2016 and re-elected in 2022; previously deputy county attorney, about a decade in private practice (family law and criminal defense) and elected Flathead County justice of the peace in 2010. source: ktvh.com (unreviewed) J.D., University of Minnesota Law School, 1993. source: ballotpedia.org (unreviewed) Ran for the Montana Supreme Court in 2024 and lost to Katherine Bidegaray, 45.9% to 54.1%. source: ballotpedia.org (unreviewed) Won a spot on the general ballot in the June 2, 2026 primary with 48.60% (132,503 votes). source: en.wikipedia.org (unreviewed) Full profile.
