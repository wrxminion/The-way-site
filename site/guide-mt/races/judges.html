Judges on the ballot

One Montana Supreme Court seat (Justice #4) is on the ballot, with two candidates. Judges run without a party label. District court judges and local justices of the peace are on some local ballots; they are listed further down.

Names, parties and ballot order are as recorded in our candidate profiles, compiled on October 15, 2026 from the Secretary of State's list. Background lines come from the pages linked on each line. A source marked "(unreviewed)" was not independently reviewed. It links to a news or reference page, not an official one. Candidate order: Eddy is listed first, Wilson second.

  • Dan Wilson source: Montana Secretary of StateNow: District judge, Montana 11th Judicial District (Flathead County); elected 2016. County, district and local judges

Coverage: the 12 district court contests listed on the Montana Secretary of State's November 3 results page, plus the Cascade County justice of the peace list certified August 20, 2026 and a news list of Gallatin County filings from March 2026. Vote counts are left out. Not covered: Yellowstone and Missoula County justices of the peace (we found no 2026 list; Yellowstone's county page still showed its 2022 list), other counties' justices of the peace, city judges and municipal courts, and water, workers' compensation and other special courts. Montana district judges are chosen in nonpartisan elections. A sitting judge with no opponent appears on a yes or no retention question.

Justices of the peace
