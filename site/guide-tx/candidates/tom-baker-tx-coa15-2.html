Candidate · 15th Court of Appeals, Place 2

Tom Baker

What this job does: One of three justices on the 15th Court of Appeals, a statewide appeals court that began in 2024. It hears civil appeals involving the state government, its agencies and officials, challenges to whether a state law is constitutional, and appeals from the state's business courts. Its rulings can be reviewed by the Texas Supreme Court. A term is six years.

Democratic · listed 2 of 2 on the Texas Secretary of State's list · on the ballot as "TOM BAKER"
