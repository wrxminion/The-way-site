Judges by county

County, district and local judge races for 221 Texas counties not shown on the main judges page. Counties are in alphabetical order. Races are partisan. Your official sample ballot is the final word.

What this covers. The 221 counties below, all from the Texas Secretary of State ballot certification dated August 28, 2026 (source: SOS ballot certification), read Nov. 4, 2026. The other 33 counties and the regional courts of appeals are on the main judges page. The same limits apply: write-ins and changes after August 28 are not reflected, we did not check each county's sample ballot, and city and municipal judges are not covered. Counties with no judge seat on the certification say so. We did not research any candidate.
