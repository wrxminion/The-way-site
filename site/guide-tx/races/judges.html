Judges on the ballot

Texans vote in partisan races for 10 statewide judgeships: four Texas Supreme Court seats (civil cases), three Court of Criminal Appeals seats (criminal cases) and three seats on the 15th Court of Appeals, a statewide court created in 2024. Each candidate appears with a party label. Local district and county judges are on county ballots and are not covered here.

Twenty-one candidates are listed here. Sixteen come from our candidate profiles (names and ballot order as recorded from the Secretary of State's list on October 15, 2026). Five do not have profiles yet (Triana, Ash, Yeary, Zimmerer, Meyer) and come from KUT and Texas Tribune reporting only. We could not open the Secretary of State's own list page from this build. Sources marked "(unreviewed)" were not independently reviewed. They link to news or reference pages.
