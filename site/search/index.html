    Search research resources

    These links leave this site and open another service's own search. Michi does not index their results, and what you see there is not reviewed by us. Your search above (and any place or years you pick) is carried into each link.

    Query sent:

    • Open Science.gov search U.S. government science and research. Free. A discovery tool, not proof of peer review. Science.gov does not take a query in the link, so copy the query above and paste it into its Full Record box.
    • Search Ballotpedia Elections, ballot measures and officeholders. Free. Check legal and election claims against official records.
    • Open CQ Researcher Issue reports with bibliographies. Needs a library or institutional login. The link opens CQ Press (Sage), and you reach CQ Researcher by signing in through your library, then searching inside it. We cannot pass a query to it.

    Ideas for wording a search

    • Use the exact measure name or number, then the state, then "fiscal note" or "Blue Book".
    • For research, try the topic with "evaluation", "synthetic control" or "implementation".
    • For primary sources, add the agency name, such as "Legislative Council" or "Secretary of State".
    • TABOR examples: "Colorado TABOR fiscal outcomes", "tax and expenditure limitations synthetic control", "Referendum C implementation". These are suggested search terms, not claims about what you will find.

    Finding a source elsewhere does not verify it. Saving sources to a review queue is not built yet.
