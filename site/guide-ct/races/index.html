The 197 races and 359 candidates come from the Secretary of the State’s 2026 General Election Guide, Appendix II ↗ (Governor and Lieutenant Governor, Secretary of the State, Comptroller, Treasurer, Attorney General, U.S. House, State Senate, State House). That list is not dated, so a candidate who withdrew or was added after it was printed is not seen here. Four people who were on town sample ballots but are not in the appendix are not shown: State House 49 (Susan Johnson), 78 (Joe Hoxha), 139 (Mark Adams) and 148 (Ensly Maizo). Names are spelled as the appendix prints them (usually without middle initials), which can differ from the ballot. Judge of Probate, Registrar of Voters, town races and local questions are not in this guide. Check your town’s sample ballot.

Connecticut elects six statewide officers in 2026: Governor and Lieutenant Governor (one team), Secretary of the State, Treasurer, Comptroller, and Attorney General. Each serves 4 years. There is no U.S. Senate race this year.

U.S. House

A U.S. Representative serves a 2-year term. Representatives write and vote on national laws and help people in their district with federal issues. Connecticut has 5 seats, all on the ballot.
