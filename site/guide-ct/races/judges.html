Judges on the ballot

We did not find any state judge or justice on Connecticut's November 3, 2026 ballot. The governor nominates judges from a commission list and the General Assembly confirms them. County-level probate judges are elected.

Connecticut state judges are appointed, so no state judge or justice is on the ballot. Probate judges are elected. The Probate Court Administrator's memo to candidates confirms a November 3, 2026 election of judge of probate. Names for 21 towns are in the section below; the rest of the 54 probate districts are not covered.

What we found

  • Selection. The governor nominates from a list compiled by the Judicial Selection Commission, and the General Assembly approves. Judges serve eight-year terms and must be renominated and reapproved. source: CT Office of Legislative Research (2012) source: ballotpedia.org (unreviewed)
  • Probate judges. Elected in partisan races in 2026. The Probate Court Administrator addressed a memo to candidates for the November 3, 2026 election. source: CT Probate Court Administrator memo Names are listed by town below.

County, district and local judges

What this covers. The judge of probate race as it appears on the official November 3, 2026 sample ballots that the Connecticut Secretary of the State posts for each town. We read the sample ballot for 21 towns, mostly the largest, on November 4, 2026. Each town's name is where that candidate appears; a probate district can cover more than one town, and we do not name districts. A person shown with two parties is on both party lines. What it does not cover. Connecticut has 54 probate districts and we reached only the districts that include these 21 towns. We could not find a single state list of all probate candidates. Some districts held special elections earlier; we did not check those. We read only one voting district's sample ballot per town, so another ballot style in the same town could differ. If your town is not here, check your own sample ballot at MyVote.CT.gov.
