Judges on the ballot

Missouri's appeals court judges, and some trial judges, are picked under the Missouri Nonpartisan Court Plan: a commission names three finalists, the governor picks one, and voters later decide whether to keep that judge. There is no opponent and no party label; you vote yes or no. A judge needs a majority of yes votes to stay for a full term. This year the certified list includes retention votes for circuit and associate circuit judges in circuits 6, 7, 16, 21, 22 and 31.

Judges on your ballot

The Judicial Performance Review Committee is an independent statewide committee supported administratively by The Missouri Bar. For 2026 it recommends retention for each judge below, per its site source: yourmissourijudges.org. Circuit and associate circuit judges in circuits 6, 7, 16, 21, 22 and 31 are listed below.

County, district and local judges

Coverage, as of November 4, 2026. Statewide: the Missouri Secretary of State 2026 General Election certified candidate list names every circuit judge on the ballot: 44 retention judges in circuits 6, 7, 16, 21, 22 and 31 (listed in the section above, matching our list name for name) and nine circuit judges in partisan races in other circuits (below). Associate circuit judges in partisan circuits are certified by each county election authority and are not on the state list. Counties checked: St. Charles County (Circuit 11), where the county voter guide names the six associate circuit judge candidates below. Jackson (16th), St. Louis County (21st), City of St. Louis (22nd) and Greene (31st) elect all their circuit and associate circuit judges by retention, so those names are the retention judges above. Not covered: associate circuit judges in every other partisan circuit and county (for example Jefferson, Franklin, Boone, Jasper, Buchanan and Cole), which we did not check. Some local judges on your ballot may be missing. We did not check write-ins.
