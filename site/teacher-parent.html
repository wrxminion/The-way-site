For teachers and parents

Meet Bubble Steve.

Bubble Steve is a scuba diver, and he hunts the hallucination shark. A hallucination is when an AI tool states something that sounds right and is not. The shark ate the facts, and Steve wants them back.

This page is for the grown-ups. It tells you what we hope to build for kids, and it asks for your help building it.

01 / The lessons

What Bubble Steve will teach.

Each lesson will be short, plain and sourced. This list shows the plan. Only the first lesson is set, and the others may change.

Lesson 1 · Coming first

Basic media literacy

The first questions anyone can ask about something they read, watch or hear online.

What it will cover
  • Who made this, and why?
  • What is a fact, and what is an opinion?
  • Can I find a second place that says the same thing?
  • Why can an AI answer sound sure and still be wrong?

02 / The goal

We want learning to be fun.

We want to build a playful place to learn, with Bubble Steve as the guide. We have not built it yet, and we want to get it right. The lessons will say plainly when something was written with AI, and they will show their sources, like the rest of the site.

This page does not collect anything from children, and we do not plan accounts for children.
