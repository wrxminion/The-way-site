Meet Scuba Steve.

This is Steve. Scuba Steve is a marine biologist who collects facts. Everything in his bag is a true paper with a named source, such as NOAA, so you can follow the link and check it yourself. He carries a suction-cup tracker launcher, not a spear gun.

This is Bruce. Bruce is the hallucination shark, a random AI hallucination and a factarian. He only eats papers, and he makes up facts that sound right. He never eats Steve. The name Bruce is just for now. A hallucination is when an AI tool states something that sounds right and is not.

This page is for the grown-ups. It tells you what we hope to build for kids, and it asks for your help building it.

01 / The lessons

What Scuba Steve will teach.

Each lesson will be short, plain and sourced. This list shows the plan. Only the first lesson is set, and the others may change.

Lesson 1 · Coming first

Basic media literacy

The first questions anyone can ask about something they read, watch or hear online.

What it will cover
  • Who made this, and why?
  • What is a fact, and what is an opinion?
  • Can I find a second place that says the same thing?
  • Why can an AI answer sound sure and still be wrong?

02 / The goal

We want learning to be fun.

We want to build a playful place to learn, with Scuba Steve as the guide. We have not built it yet, and we want to get it right. The lessons will say plainly when something was written with AI, and they will show their sources, like the rest of the site.

This page does not collect anything from children. We want to talk with educators about the direction they want us to go with this, so tell us what would help you.
