Judges up for retention

The Secretary of State's publicity pamphlet lists 82 judges and justices on the retention ballot: one Arizona Supreme Court justice, 14 Court of Appeals judges and Superior Court judges in Coconino, Pinal, Pima and Maricopa counties. Voters decide whether each stays in office (yes or no). The pamphlet prints "meets judicial standards" for every one. INCOMPLETE: the retention question wording was not seen, and Superior Court judges in the other 11 counties are not covered by the pamphlet.

  • The Judicial Performance Review Commission considers and votes on whether a judge "MEETS" or "DOES NOT MEET" Judicial Performance Standards. The pamphlet prints "MEETS JUDICIAL STANDARDS" for every judge below and lists no one under "DO NOT MEET". source: apps.azsos.gov (Arizona Secretary of State)
