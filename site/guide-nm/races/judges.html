Judges on the ballot

In New Mexico, judges first win a seat in a partisan election; later they face a yes or no retention vote. Retention votes for the judges on your ballot are listed here, from the Secretary of State's candidate list. The list does not say which court the three statewide retention judges sit on. District, Metropolitan Court, magistrate and municipal judge races are on each county's page.
