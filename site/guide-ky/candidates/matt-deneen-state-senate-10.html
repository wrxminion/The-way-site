Candidate · State Senate, District 10

Matt Deneen

What this job does: Your district's vote in the Kentucky Senate. Senators write and vote on state laws and the state budget, approve some of the governor's appointments, and with the House can override the governor's veto with a majority of each chamber. A term is four years.

Background

  • Deneen has served in the Kentucky Senate since 2023 and chairs the Senate Veterans, Military Affairs, and Public Protection Committee. source: legislature.ky.gov
  • Deneen earned a B.A. from Georgetown College and an M.A. from Western Kentucky University. source: legislature.ky.gov
  • Deneen is a former Hardin County Schools teacher and former Elizabethtown city council member. source: branch.vote
  • Deneen has served on the Elizabethtown Airport Board and chaired the Elizabethtown Trail Town Advisory Board. source: legislature.ky.gov
