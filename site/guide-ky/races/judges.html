Judges on the ballot

Kentucky elects judges by district in nonpartisan races. This year one Kentucky Supreme Court seat (3rd District) and two Court of Appeals seats (3rd District, 2nd Division and 5th District, 1st Division) are on the ballot, and only voters in those districts see them. Circuit, family court and district judges follow in the last section.

We checked the Secretary of State filing page on November 4, 2026. It lists one Supreme Court candidate and three Court of Appeals candidates: Greg Lay alone for the 3rd District, 2nd Division, and Lucy Ferguson VanMeter and Will Moynahan for the 5th District, 1st Division. Our earlier version listed only VanMeter for the 5th District. Background lines link to news or campaign pages and are marked "(unreviewed)", meaning not independently reviewed, unless they are official.

Kentucky Supreme Court, 3rd District

Only the 3rd District votes on this seat. Kentucky Supreme Court and Court of Appeals judges are elected by district in nonpartisan races.

  • Debra Hembree Lambert source: KY Secretary of State filingsJustice of the Supreme Court, 3rd District, nonpartisan. The Secretary of State filing page lists her as the only candidate for this seat (filed 11/10/2025).

Court of Appeals, 3rd District, 2nd Division (nonpartisan, only in that district)

  • Greg Lay source: Kentucky State Board of ElectionsNow: Circuit Judge, 27th Judicial Circuit (Knox and Laurel counties) (since 2004 election). Lay was elected circuit judge for the 27th Judicial Circuit (Knox and Laurel counties) in 2004 and serves as chief circuit judge. source: sentinel-echo.com (unreviewed) Lay is chief regional circuit judge for the Cumberland Region. source: sentinel-echo.com (unreviewed) The Court of Appeals seat was vacated by the retirement of Judge James Lambert. source: sentinel-echo.com (unreviewed) Full profile.

County, district and local judges

Coverage: this section lists every Circuit Judge, Family Court Circuit Judge and District Judge filing shown on the Kentucky Secretary of State candidate filings page for the 2026 general election, as we read it on November 4, 2026. That page showed 4 circuit, 5 family court and 126 district judge filings, plus 3 Court of Appeals filings and 1 Supreme Court filing. The page does not give county names for districts and divisions. Match a district to your county on your sample ballot or the Kentucky Court of Justice site.

NOT covered: we did not check county clerk records or any filing made only with a county clerk. In the 30th District, 15th Division, the page shows a write-in filing for Michael R. Wilson. It also lists a withdrawn filing for Mary Jude Wolford, who is not shown as a candidate.

How Kentucky fills these seats: judges are elected in nonpartisan races. These are contested elections, not retention votes. A seat with one name had one filer on the state page and is unopposed there. The governor fills a vacancy by appointment until the next election.
