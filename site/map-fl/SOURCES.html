Where the map comes from

District lines

U.S. Census Bureau 2024 cartographic boundary files (1:500,000): Florida Senate file EOGPCRP2026 (the 2026 U.S. House map; the Census file still shows the old map) for the 28 U.S. House districts, cb_2024_12_sldu_500k for the 40 state Senate districts, cb_2024_12_sldl_500k for the 120 state House districts, cb_2024_us_county_500k for counties and cb_2024_12_place_500k for city and town limits. These show the Florida Legislature's 2022 state Senate and House maps. District files are converted without simplification; county outlines and city limits are simplified for display. Near a boundary line they can be off by a block or more.
