Judges on the ballot

In Illinois, judges on the retention ballot get a yes or no vote with no opponent. This page lists the judges on the ballot for Cook County, the downstate circuits and the Appellate Court, from official lists. Check your sample ballot for your own seats.

Coverage. Cook County is covered from the Cook County Clerk's candidate list. Downstate circuit retention and vacancy races and Appellate Court retention are covered from the State Board of Elections candidate filing search, names only. Individual judges were not reviewed. No Illinois Supreme Court seat was found. See the coverage note under County, district and local judges. Your county clerk's sample ballot is the final word.

How retention works

  • Yes or no, no opponent. A judge on the retention ballot is kept if enough voters say yes. Illinois Policy reports a judge needs a yes from 60 percent of voters. source: Illinois Policy, Cook retention

We did not independently review any downstate judge or candidate. Your county clerk's sample ballot is the final word.

Illinois circuit judges are elected in partisan races to fill a vacancy. After that they face a yes or no retention vote and need three-fifths yes to stay. Every race below shows one candidate (a Democrat) in the sources we read.
