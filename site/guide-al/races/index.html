  • Secretary of State2 candidates

  • State Treasurer2 candidates

    Manages and invests the state's money and runs the state's college savings and unclaimed property programs.

  • State Auditor2 candidates

    Keeps track of state-owned property (like furniture, vehicles and equipment in state agencies) and serves on the state's board that appoints county voter registrars.

  • Commissioner of Agriculture and Industries2 candidates

    Runs the state Department of Agriculture and Industries, which checks food safety, gas pumps and store scales, and supports farmers.

Statewide, voters choose a governor, lieutenant governor, attorney general, secretary of state, treasurer, auditor, agriculture commissioner, two Public Service Commission seats, a U.S. senator, and judges for the Alabama Supreme Court, Court of Civil Appeals and Court of Criminal Appeals. Judges in Alabama run in partisan races, like other offices. U.S. House Districts 1, 2, 6 and 7 are held back from the list below: Alabama held special primaries on August 11, 2026 under the new map, and the official Republican nominee for each of those districts is not in the state certifications we have. Check your sample ballot for those four districts.
