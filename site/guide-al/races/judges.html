Judges on the ballot

Alabama elects its appellate judges in partisan races. Two Supreme Court seats (Places 7 and 8), two Court of Civil Appeals seats and two Court of Criminal Appeals seats are on the November 3, 2026 ballot. Only Supreme Court Place 8 has two candidates. Circuit and district judges are also elected; they are listed below the statewide courts.

Checked on November 4, 2026 against the Alabama Secretary of State's General Election certifications of August 26, 2026 (Republican, Democratic and independent lists). Those PDFs are scanned images, so we read them with text recognition. Spellings could be off by a letter; your sample ballot is the final word.

County, district and local judges

What this covers. Alabama circuit judges and district judges are elected in partisan races. This section lists every circuit and district judge seat that appears on the Secretary of State's General Election certifications dated August 26, 2026 (Republican and Democratic parties). The independent list has no judges. A seat shows only the party nominees certified; a write-in is always possible. Not covered: probate judges (the certifications list none, so we found none on the 2026 ballot), municipal court judges, and any seat with no party nominee. The certifications list circuit numbers, not counties, so we print the circuit number as listed. Names were read from scanned PDFs by text recognition and are not independently reviewed. Seats that are not listed have no candidate on those certifications.
