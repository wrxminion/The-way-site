How to read a result

  • Certified by the state The official, final result. Only the state or county can certify.
  • What AP is calling (not certified) The Associated Press expects this side to win. It is a news call, not an official result.
  • State shows leader (not certified) The state's own results page marks who is ahead in the count so far. It is not a call.
  • Not called, not certified No one has called it yet. The numbers are the count so far.
