Judges on the ballot

In Iowa, voters decide yes or no on whether to keep appointed judges. There are no opponents. Three Court of Appeals judges are on every ballot, and 84 trial court judges appear in their own judicial election districts.

Check your sample ballot. The judge names and districts below come from the Iowa State Bar Association's 2026 Judicial Performance Review (a bar association guide, not a state election list). The Iowa Judicial Branch confirms the counts: 3 Court of Appeals judges and 84 trial court judges. Your county auditor's sample ballot is the final word on which judges you vote on.
