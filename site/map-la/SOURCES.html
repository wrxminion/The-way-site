Where the map comes from

District lines

U.S. Census Bureau 2024 cartographic boundary files (1:500,000): the Louisiana Legislature's Act 2 (2026 Regular Session) congressional plan shapefile (from redist.legis.la.gov), simplified slightly for display, for the 6 U.S. House districts, cb_2024_22_sldu_500k for the 39 state Senate districts, cb_2024_22_sldl_500k for the 105 state House districts, cb_2024_us_county_500k for counties and cb_2024_22_place_500k for city and town limits. These show the Only one legislative race on Nov. 3, 2026: State Representative District 92 (special election). (Note: SB 25 of the 2026 session redrew 3 state senate districts for future elections.). District files are converted without simplification; county outlines and city limits are simplified for display. Near a boundary line they can be off by a block or more.
