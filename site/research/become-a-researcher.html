Research

Become a researcher!

Reading sources and comparing them with claims is work a person should do. There is more of it than a few of us can manage. If you can read carefully, you can help.

AI finds sources. People decide what is checked.

What researchers do

Review

Open an item marked "needs review" and read it. Move its label up the ladder as far as you have really gone: skimmed, read, under review, or confirmed.

Check sources

Open each saved source. Does it say what the page says it says? Is the quote exact? Is the date right? Note what matches and what does not.

Run the claim checks

Every claim needs at least two sources, and at least one must be a primary record, such as a law, a court filing, a vote record, a budget or a transcript.

Look at both sides

Write down the strongest case against the claim, and what the sources say in answer. If the sources do not answer it, say so.

The claim checks, in order

  1. Find the exact words of the claim.
  2. Find two sources that bear on it. At least one is a primary record.
  3. Check each quote and number against the source itself.
  4. Look for the strongest case on the other side, and read its sources too.
  5. Read the challenge check, if there is one. A second AI may have flagged weak sources or missing counterarguments. Treat each flag as a lead, then confirm it in the sources yourself.
  6. Pick the label that matches what you did, not what you hope is true.

Your checks are on the record

Each package comes with a source check record that readers can open. It shows which sources a person checked, when, and a saved copy of each source as it looked that day. Your work is what fills it in, so note what you checked and when, and save the source.

What researchers do not do

  • You do not decide whether a candidate or party is good or bad. Research is about policies and records.
  • You never make up a quote or a source. That costs points and is recorded.
  • You do not let AI settle a fact. AI can point you to a document. Only the document says what it says.
