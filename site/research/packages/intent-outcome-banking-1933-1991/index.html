Summary

Established: dates and main provisions of five federal banking laws, and headline numbers from the FDIC and the Federal Reserve (for example, about 9,000 bank suspensions from 1930 to 1933).

Why it matters: it shows what lawmakers said they were fixing next to what the records show followed.

Unresolved: the number of 1934 failures and an exact start date of deposit insurance were not found on the pages checked; long-run effects of the 1989 and 1991 reforms need independent studies.

Methodology and limits

Each statement in source_registry.csv is marked confirmed (found in a cited source) or unresolved (not found; that is not the same as false). Only two source families were searched. Outcome sentences are summaries, not measurements. Read the full README and the data dictionary. Version history: CHANGELOG.md. Older versions will stay available.

Reproduce our findings

There is no script, because the checks were made by hand. Open the pages listed in the registry and compare each claim. That is the whole method.
