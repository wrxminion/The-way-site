# Intent vs. Outcome: banking rules, 1933 to 1991 (v1.0.0)
Released 2026-10-25. Status: DRAFT. Not publication-verified.

## Question
For four federal banking laws, what did the law say it would do, and what do the cited records show happened?
This package does not say any law caused any later event.

## Scope
Banking Act of 1933 (deposit insurance), DIDMCA 1980, Garn-St Germain 1982, FIRREA 1989 and FDICIA 1991. United States only.

## Method
A person compared each statement with the cited FDIC and Federal Reserve History pages on 2026-10-25 and recorded whether it was found (`confirmed`) or not found (`unresolved`). Source text was not copied; it is paraphrased and linked.
Original drafting came from an outside AI-assisted handoff and is treated as untrusted until a person checks it.

## Files
- `events.csv`, `events.json`: the four events (same data).
- `source_registry.csv`: every checkable claim with its evidence class.
- `schema.json`: data dictionary.
- `CHANGELOG.md`, `SHA256SUMS.txt`.

## Known limits
Only two source families were searched. Outcome sentences are summaries, not measurements. "Unresolved" means we did not find it on the page checked.

## Rights
Facts come from U.S. government and Federal Reserve pages that we link rather than copy. The license for our own compilation has not been set by the Michi Institute yet, so reuse beyond reading and citing is not yet granted.
