News snapshots

Pick a day, or a short range, and download the news feed as it stood then: headlines, publishers, links, tags, AI labels and whether a person has checked the item. Headlines and links only, no article text. Times show when we first saw an item.

Each day shows up to 200 items, with the ones tied to ballot measures and candidates first. Full-day downloads for researchers (an academic or journalist login) are coming soon. Join The Way and we will tell you inside the site when they open.

Filters narrow the up to 200 items each day holds. They run in your browser before the file is saved.
