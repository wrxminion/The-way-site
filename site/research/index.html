How a piece of research gets made

Yes, AI is used here. It never creates facts. It finds sources and suggests where to dig, and a person decides what is checked. Here is the path, step by step.

  1. A person

    A person has an idea

    A member asks a question or spots a claim worth looking into. It is about a policy or a record, never about whether a candidate or party is good or bad.

  2. AI helps

    AI refines it and finds sources

    AI sharpens the question, looks for documents that bear on it, and suggests deeper dives. It does not write facts. A fact is what a source says, and the source stays exactly as it is.

  3. AI helps

    A second AI runs a challenge check

    A different AI argues the other side. It hunts for weak or missing sources and flags counterarguments the first pass left out. It writes no facts and gives no verdict. Its flags are leads for the reviewer, and the reviewer decides which ones matter.

  4. AI helps

    It is posted as "needs review"

    The sources are organized and saved with their dates, so anyone can open them. The page carries the label AI written, unchecked until a person works on it.

  5. A person

    A person reviews it

    A member compares each claim with its sources. Each claim needs at least two sources, one of them a primary record, and we look at the strongest case on both sides. The label moves up the ladder as the work is done.

  6. A person

    Posted as human reviewed, added to the research packages

    Only when a person has checked every source does the page say Confirmed accurate by a human. Then it joins the research packages below. Readers also get the source check record for each package: which sources a person checked, when, and a saved copy of each source as it looked that day. If no person has finished, the page says so plainly.

  1. Unchecked
  2. Skimmed
  3. Read
  4. Under review
  5. Confirmed by human
