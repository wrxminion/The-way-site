Judges on the ballot

We did not find any state judge or justice on Maine's November 3, 2026 ballot. Maine's judges are appointed. County probate judges are the exception and are elected.

Statewide: the Maine Secretary of State's 2026 general election candidate list has no state judge or justice on it. It has candidates for U.S. Senate, U.S. House, governor, legislature and county offices. Judges of probate are the only elected judges, and they are in the county section below. Everything else about judicial selection on this page comes from Ballotpedia and is not independently reviewed.

What we found

  • Supreme Judicial Court. A nominating commission sends the governor a list, the governor is not required to choose from it, and a majority of the state Senate must confirm. Justices serve seven-year terms. source: ballotpedia.org (unreviewed)
  • Probate judges. Judges of probate are elected by county in partisan races. Candidates for 2026 are listed by county below. source: Maine SOS candidate list

County, district and local judges

What this covers. Judges of probate in the seven Maine counties that have a candidate on the Maine Secretary of State's 2026 General Election Candidates List (final version, an Excel file posted on the Secretary of State's Upcoming Elections page). We read it on November 4, 2026. It lists primary winners and nonparty candidates. The counties are Androscoggin, Cumberland, Franklin, Hancock, Knox, Penobscot and Washington. The list has no judge of probate candidate for the other nine counties, so we show none for them. What it does not cover. Declared write-in candidates are on a separate state list that we did not read. The state's separate list of special county elections is not covered, because we could not open it. Other Maine judges, including district and superior court judges, are appointed and are not on the ballot.

  • Androscoggin County: Michael Dubois (Republican) source: Maine SOS candidate listLives in Lewiston. Only candidate listed for judge of probate.
  • Cumberland County: Paul Aranson (Democratic) source: Maine SOS candidate listLives in Scarborough. Only candidate listed for judge of probate.
  • Franklin County: Margot Joly (Democratic) source: Maine SOS candidate listLives in Weld. Only candidate listed for judge of probate.
  • Hancock County: Matthew J. Foster (Republican) source: Maine SOS candidate listLives in Hancock. One of two candidates listed for judge of probate; the other is Brian Thomas.
  • Hancock County: Brian Thomas (Democratic) source: Maine SOS candidate listLives in Lamoine. One of two candidates listed for judge of probate; the other is Matthew J. Foster.
  • Knox County: Carol R. Emery (Republican) source: Maine SOS candidate listLives in St. George. Only candidate listed for judge of probate.
  • Penobscot County: Beth L. Seaney (Republican) source: Maine SOS candidate listLives in Hermon. Only candidate listed for judge of probate.
  • Washington County: Dennis L. Mahar (Democratic) source: Maine SOS candidate listLives in Pembroke. Only candidate listed for judge of probate.

Judges of probate hear wills, estates, guardianships and similar cases. These are part of the county ballot, so a county race may be absent from your sample ballot if your county is not listed above. source: Maine SOS elections page
