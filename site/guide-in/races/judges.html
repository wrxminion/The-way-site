Judges on the retention ballot

Appellate judges in Indiana don't run against opponents. Voters decide whether each one stays in office (yes or no). Local trial judges in most counties do run against opponents, and they are listed in the last section.

Judges on your ballot

County, district and local judges

Coverage: this section lists every judge of a circuit court, superior court and Marion County small claims court that appears on the Indiana Election Division general election candidate list made September 22, 2026. That list names the county, court and seat for each candidate and the candidate’s party. We did not read other lists.

NOT covered: city and town courts other than the one noted below, and any county whose superior or circuit judges are not elected or have no candidate on this list. Indiana counties where judges are not on the list are not shown here. Marion County superior courts and Lake County superior courts do not appear on this list, so we do not list them. The Indiana courts site says judges in some counties are chosen by a nominating commission and the governor, then stand for retention. We have not checked that county by county.

How these seats work: where the list shows one name for a seat, that candidate is unopposed on that list. Where it shows two or more, the race is contested. Allen and Vanderburgh Superior Court races are listed as nonpartisan. Most others carry a party label.

City courts: the list also shows Mark W. Mason (Republican) for Attica City Judge. We did not search for other city or town judges.
