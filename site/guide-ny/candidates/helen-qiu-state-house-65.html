Candidate · State Assembly, District 65

Helen Qiu

What this job does: Your district's vote in the New York State Assembly (150 members), the larger house of the Legislature. Members write and vote on state laws and the state budget. A term is two years.

Republican · listed 2 of 2 on the State Board of Elections' list · on the ballot as "Helen Qiu"

Occupation
Pastor

Background

  • Bachelor's degree from Zhongshan University (1990); graduate degrees from Reformed Seminary (2007) and Columbia University (2010), per Ballotpedia. source: ballotpedia.org
  • Worked as a teacher, engineer and engineering manager before becoming a pastor, per Ballotpedia. source: ballotpedia.org
  • Republican nominee for Assembly District 65 in 2022; lost to Grace Lee. source: ballotpedia.org
  • Ran for NYC Council District 1 in 2023 and 2025; lost both general elections to Christopher Marte. source: ballotpedia.org
