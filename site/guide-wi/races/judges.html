Judges on the ballot

We did not find any Wisconsin Supreme Court seat on the November 3, 2026 ballot. The justice seat was decided in the April 7, 2026 election.

The Wisconsin court system says Chris Taylor will take a 10-year term on the Supreme Court beginning August 1, 2026. News reports say Taylor defeated Maria Lazar on April 7. The court system's spring election list shows the Supreme Court, Court of Appeals and circuit court judge races all on the April 7 ballot. Details are in the last section.

What we found

  • Court system. The Wisconsin court system's April 2026 newsletter covers the Supreme Court election and the new term. source: Wisconsin Court System
  • Result. Minnesota Lawyer reports Taylor won decisively, expanding the liberal majority from 4-3 to 5-2. The report is unofficial; we could not confirm certified results. source: minnlawyer.com (unreviewed)

County, district and local judges

Coverage: we read the Wisconsin court system article on the April 7, 2026 spring judicial elections. It lists the Supreme Court seat, three Court of Appeals seats and circuit court branches in several counties, and it names no judicial race on a November ballot. We did not read the Wisconsin Elections Commission November candidate list. So we found no judge on the November 3, 2026 ballot, but we did not confirm that from the commission.

How Wisconsin elects judges: the Supreme Court, Court of Appeals and circuit court judges are elected in the spring election in April, not in November. These April races were contested or unopposed elections, not retention votes. We did not check municipal court judges or any November special election, and we do not know how vacancies are filled in each court.
