Where the map comes from

District lines

U.S. Census Bureau 2024 cartographic boundary files (1:500,000): the Texas Legislative Council's PLANC2333 shapefile for the 38 U.S. House districts, cb_2024_48_sldu_500k for the 31 state Senate districts, cb_2024_48_sldl_500k for the 150 state House districts, cb_2024_us_county_500k for counties and cb_2024_48_place_500k for city and town limits. These show the Texas Legislature's 2021 state Senate and House maps (adopted again in 2023). District files are converted without simplification; county outlines and city limits are simplified for display. Near a boundary line they can be off by a block or more.

Files

  • cb_2024_48_sldu_500k.zip: www2.census.gov, retrieved 2026-10-13
  • cb_2024_48_sldl_500k.zip: www2.census.gov, retrieved 2026-10-13
  • cb_2024_48_place_500k.zip: www2.census.gov, retrieved 2026-10-13
  • tx.csv: data.openstates.org, retrieved 2026-10-13
  • planc2333.zip: data.capitol.texas.gov, retrieved 2026-10-13
  • 2026 Ballot Certification-final.pdf: Texas Secretary of State (official file), retrieved 2026-10-13
  • 2026 Sample Ballot-final.pdf: Texas Secretary of State (official file), retrieved 2026-10-13

Census files: www2.census.gov/geo/tiger/GENZ2024/shp/.
