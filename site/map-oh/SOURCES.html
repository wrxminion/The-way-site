Where the map comes from

District lines

U.S. Census Bureau 2024 cartographic boundary files (1:500,000): cb_2024_us_cd119_500k (Ohio features only; these are the old lines, not the 2026 map) for the 15 U.S. House districts, cb_2024_39_sldu_500k for the 33 state Senate districts, cb_2024_39_sldl_500k for the 99 state House districts, cb_2024_us_county_500k for counties and cb_2024_39_place_500k for city and town limits. These show the Ohio Redistricting Commission's 2023 Ohio Senate and Ohio House maps. District files are converted without simplification; county outlines and city limits are simplified for display. Near a boundary line they can be off by a block or more.
