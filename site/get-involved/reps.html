Contact your representatives

Everyone in a state has two U.S. senators and one member of the U.S. House. D.C. and the territories have a delegate or resident commissioner in the House instead. Pick your state to see phone numbers, websites and contact forms.

Not sure which House district you live in? Find your district by address.

Tips that help your message count. Say you live in their state or district and give your town. Name the bill (for example, H.R. 7954) and say yes or no. Keep it short and polite. Most contact forms accept only people who live in that state or district, and many ask for your street address.

House members' current terms end January 3, 2027. The winners of the November 3, 2026 election take office then, and this list will change.
