Contact Your Senator

Phone and official contact form for all 100 senators. Pick your senator, and the list shows what fits them: bills coming up, their committees, and how they voted on recent bills, so you can thank them or say you disagree. Search any topic, sort by National, State or Local, or say it in your own words (a list or rough notes is fine), and get a letter or a call script. Several issues can go in one letter, and you can share it with a friend. Nothing you type is saved or sent anywhere.
