The yearly defense bill (NDAA) for 2027

When

No vote date set yet.

What it is

Every year Congress passes a National Defense Authorization Act (NDAA) that sets defense policy and spending levels. For 2027 there are two versions.

The House bill, H.R. 8800, was received in the Senate on September 14, 2026, according to the Library of Congress record.

The Senate bill, S. 4784, sponsored by Senator Roger Wicker of Mississippi, was approved by the Senate Armed Services Committee. On July 14, 2026 the Senate failed to end debate on taking it up, 50 to 46; ending debate needs three fifths of senators.

No vote is scheduled on the pages we read. News analysis expects the House and Senate committees to try to agree on one version in the coming weeks.
