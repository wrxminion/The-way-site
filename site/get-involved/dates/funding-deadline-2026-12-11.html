Deadline · Federal funding

Government funding runs out, December 11

When

Friday, December 11, 2026 (funding ends at the end of the day).

What it is

The law that funds the federal government right now is a stopgap, Public Law 119-103, the Continuing Appropriations and Extensions Act, 2027 (H.R. 6500), signed September 2, 2026. Section 106(3) says the money lasts through December 11, 2026.

Unless Congress passes new funding by then, parts of the government shut down. The same law also extends the National Flood Insurance Program and surface transportation (highway) programs to December 11, so those end too unless they are extended again.

Congress can pass another stopgap, a full-year bill, or let funding lapse. Funding bills start in the Appropriations Committees of the House and Senate.

The bill: H.R. 6500
