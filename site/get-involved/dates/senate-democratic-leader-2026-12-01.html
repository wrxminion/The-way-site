Party vote coming up · Senate Democratic caucus

Senate Democrats elect their leader, December 1

When

Tuesday, December 1, 2026.

What it is

Senate Democrats and the two independents who caucus with them meet to elect their leader for the next Congress. News reports say the current leader, Chuck Schumer of New York, is challenged by Chris Van Hollen of Maryland.

It is a closed meeting with a secret ballot, so there is no public record of how each senator votes. Senators often say publicly how they plan to vote, and news outlets keep lists.

This is a party vote, not a Senate vote, so it is not on senate.gov. Every fact about the date and the race comes from news reporting.
