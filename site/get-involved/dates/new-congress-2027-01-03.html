Date · The 120th Congress

The new Congress begins, January 3, 2027

When

Sunday, January 3, 2027, at noon.

What it is

The 20th Amendment says the terms of senators and representatives end at noon on January 3 and the terms of their successors begin then.

Every House seat was on the ballot on November 3, 2026, so all 435 House terms end, and the winners take office. One third of the Senate was also up; the senators whose terms end are listed below.

Any bill that has not passed both the House and the Senate by then dies and must be introduced again in the new Congress.
