Senate votes on federal judges, November 16

When

Monday, November 16, 2026, 5:30 p.m. Eastern.

What it is

At 5:30 p.m. the Senate votes on confirming Daniel Desmond Domenico, of Colorado, to be a U.S. Circuit Judge for the Tenth Circuit.

At the same time, the motions to end debate (cloture) on four district judge nominations ripen, so the Senate can then vote on them: Richard Wesley Bennett (Southern District of Texas), Courtney Leigh Coker (Northern District of Texas), Kyle Frederick Reardon (District of Alaska) and Daniel Lewis Ballou (Eastern District of Kentucky).

A confirmation vote on Anna St. John, of Louisiana, to be a U.S. Circuit Judge for the Fifth Circuit comes at a time the Majority Leader sets with the Democratic Leader, no earlier than November 16.

The president nominates federal judges, the Senate Judiciary Committee holds hearings and votes on them, and then the full Senate votes to confirm. Judges serve for life.
