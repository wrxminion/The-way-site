Contact Your Representative

Phone and website for every member of the House. Pick your representative, then say what is on your mind in your own words (a list or rough notes is fine), or pick a vote and the facts on your side, and get a letter or a call script. Several issues can go in one letter. House Democrats vote on their leader on November 18, 2026, and the full House elects the Speaker on January 3, 2027. Nothing you type is saved or sent anywhere.
