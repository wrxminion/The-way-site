What it does

Takes three parcels of federal land (about 847 acres) in San Diego County, California, into trust for the Barona Group as part of its reservation. One parcel is managed by the Bureau of Land Management and two are Forest Service land, which would move to the Interior Department. Existing liens, rights-of-way, permits, public access and wildfire access stay in place, the Tribe must keep a fuel break on two parcels, and gaming is not allowed on the land.
