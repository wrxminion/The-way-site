House vote coming up · H.R. 3495 · 119th Congress

Direct Seller and Real Estate Agent Harmonization Act

Official title: "To amend the Fair Labor Standards Act of 1938 to clarify the definition of employee as it relates to direct sellers and real estate agents, and for other purposes."

When the vote is

This bill is listed for a House vote: Wednesday, November 18 to Friday, November 20, 2026. On Wednesday and Thursday the House meets at 10:00 a.m. for morning hour and noon for legislative business. On Friday it meets at 9:00 a.m., and last votes are expected no later than 3:00 p.m. The schedule does not say which of these days each bill comes up.

What it does

Takes real estate agents and direct sellers of consumer products out of the definition of "employee" in the Fair Labor Standards Act, the federal minimum wage and overtime law. Workers who are still employees under that law must get the minimum wage and generally get overtime pay for hours over 40 in a week.
