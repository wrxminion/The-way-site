What it does

H.R. 9237 bundles more than 60 veterans measures. It adds a benefit for combat-injured military retirees (the Major Richard Star Act). To help pay for it, Section 108 lowers disability ratings for sleep apnea and tinnitus on future claims. The bill says ratings already in effect are protected. The House passed it by voice vote on November 11, 2026, so there is no list of how each member voted. The Senate version is S. 4744.
