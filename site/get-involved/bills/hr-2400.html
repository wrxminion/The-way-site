House vote coming up · H.R. 2400 · 119th Congress

Pit River Land Transfer Act

Official title: "To take certain Federal land in the State of California into trust for the benefit of the Pit River Tribe."

When the vote is

The Senate's changes to this bill are listed for a House vote: Tuesday, November 17, 2026. The House meets at noon for morning hour and 2:00 p.m. for legislative business. Votes are postponed until 6:30 p.m.

Under suspension of the rules. Debate is short, no changes can be offered on the floor, and the bill needs two thirds of the members voting to pass. What these words mean (Congress.gov glossary) ↗

The House already passed this bill. The Senate passed it with changes, so the House now votes on the Senate's changes. The schedule calls it the "Pit River Land Transfer Act of 2026." The Library of Congress record we read (updated August 6, 2026) does not show the Senate vote yet.

From the House Majority Leader's weekly schedule and the House floor documents page, checked November 15, 2026. Schedules change. The Majority Leader's schedule says additional legislative items are possible.

What it does

Takes about 604 acres in California into trust for the Pit River Tribe: about 584 acres of Forest Service land (leaving out about 20 acres of roads and rights-of-way) and about 40 acres the Tribe already owns. The land becomes part of the reservation, and gaming is not allowed on it.

Plain-words version by The Way of the Congressional Research Service summary (version: Introduced in House, March 27, 2025). The official summary is on Congress.gov. This summary is of the bill as first written; the committee or the House may have changed it since.
