What it does

Requires federal agencies to publish a searchable online list of certain legal settlements they sign, with a copy of each one and key facts: whether it settles civil or criminal claims, the date, the laws said to be broken, lawyer fees, money paid to or by the government, how long it lasts, and any government body it names. It covers settlements of at least $10 million, ones that appoint a court monitor, ones with a state or local government, and others the Office of Management and Budget picks. Personnel matters, plea deals, non-prosecution deals and tax matters are left out. It reaches back to settlements signed since January 1, 2015 that are still in effect.
