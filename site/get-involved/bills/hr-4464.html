House vote coming up · H.R. 4464 · 119th Congress

Preventive Health Savings Act

Official title: "To amend the Congressional Budget Act of 1974 respecting the scoring of preventive health savings."

When the vote is

This bill is listed for a House vote: Wednesday, November 18 to Friday, November 20, 2026. On Wednesday and Thursday the House meets at 10:00 a.m. for morning hour and noon for legislative business. On Friday it meets at 9:00 a.m., and last votes are expected no later than 3:00 p.m. The schedule does not say which of these days each bill comes up.

What it does

Lets Congress ask the Congressional Budget Office to estimate whether a bill would save money beyond the usual 10-year budget window through preventive health care, meaning actions that protect health and prevent disease, disability and early death. If the office finds savings, it must describe and estimate them and explain how. These estimates are extra information only and cannot be used to meet budget rules.
