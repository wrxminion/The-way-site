What it does

Directs the President to put sanctions on foreign people responsible for online money scams against Americans, including a decision on 43 named foreign persons. Creates a task force across federal agencies to break up criminal groups that run large scam centers using trafficked forced labor. The task force must give Congress a plan and report every year for five years, including who was sanctioned. Lets the State Department give care, shelter and support to trafficking victims held in these centers.
