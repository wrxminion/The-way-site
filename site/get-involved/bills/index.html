How a vote works

House

Under suspension of the rules: Debate is short, no changes can be offered on the floor, and the bill needs two thirds of the members voting to pass.

Under a rule: The House first votes on a resolution (a "rule") that sets the terms for debate. The bill then needs a simple majority to pass.

Senate

Cloture: a vote to end debate so the Senate can move to a final vote. It needs three fifths of senators (60 when every seat is filled) for most bills, and a simple majority for nominations.

Nominations: the president picks judges and officials, and the Senate votes to confirm them.
