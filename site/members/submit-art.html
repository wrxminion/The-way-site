Why our pictures are AI generated for now

The Way is a new nonprofit and we have no art budget. We still want every history case and every research article to have a picture, so the pages look finished and feel alike. For now, we fill that space with AI generated images.

  • They are fillers, not the plan. Each one is labeled on its page as AI generated. We would rather show an artist’s work.
  • They carry no facts. A banner is a mood and a few plain objects. It never shows a real person, a flag, a logo or any words, and it never says who was right. The facts on a page come from sources, not from the picture.
  • A person checks each one against fixed rules: the size, no text or faces, calm colors, nothing that leans one way.
  • Real art replaces them. When we accept a piece, it replaces the AI image on that page. If more than one artist makes art for the same page, each accepted piece joins a rotation, so visitors see a different artist’s work from one visit to the next. Once a page has real art, the AI image is dropped from it.
  • Every artist is credited. Your name goes under your picture, linked to your portfolio if you give one, and on our All artists on our site page, with the pages your art is on.
  • Yes, a lot of it is AI slop, and we say so. We use a variety of language and image models, including several that run in house only. Whatever tool made a picture, a person checks it before it goes up.
  • We cannot pay for art itself right now. We say so plainly. The only exception is the optional sales box below, where you can ask for a share of what your art earns. You decide if the credit and the exposure are worth it to you, and you can ask us to take your art down at any time.

Terms for art you give us

  • Your art is freely given. We may show it on this site, and researchers and schools may use it under fair use, and in any publication or research document they produce by or through their use of the website.
  • You keep full rights to your art. You may keep using it and selling it yourself, wherever you like.
  • We will never sell, lease, rent or license any image, and nobody else may do so through us. The one exception is art whose artist ticks the separate, optional box about raising funds (see the next section).
  • No political party and no politician may use your work in their ads.
  • If your image becomes tied to one political party, or to hate or extremist imagery, we stop using it on our site.
  • You are credited by the name you give, linked to your portfolio if you add one.
  • You can ask us to take your art down at any time.

Optional: help raise funds with your art

  • This is a separate choice, and it is off unless you tick it. Saying no changes nothing about whether we use your art on the site.
  • If you say yes, The Way may put your art on products such as shirts, and show those products in places such as a billboard or an article, to raise funds for The Way.
  • You choose what happens to your share. You can ask for a share of what your art earns, with the amount agreed with you in writing before anything is sold. Or you can donate your share to a wildlife fund. The fund rotates, and we will say on this site which one is featured at the time.
  • You approve the final layout first. Nothing is made, shown or sold until you have seen and approved the exact design and where it will appear. If you say no to a layout, we do not use it.
  • You can take your yes back at any time. We stop using your art on new products. Items already sold cannot be taken back.
  • The other rules still hold. You keep your rights. We never lease, rent or license your art to anyone else. No political party or politician may use it in their ads.

What we are looking for

  • A wide picture, 1600 by 400 pixels (four times as wide as tall). We can crop a close size.
  • Your own work only. No text, no faces or likenesses of real people, no flags, no logos, no party colors.
  • Calm and neutral. The banner never says who was right.
  • PNG, JPEG or WebP, up to 8 MB. Or send a link to where it is shown.
